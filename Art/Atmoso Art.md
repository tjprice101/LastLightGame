## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu, with Flora's silhouette discipline,
not a poster, adult anime knight or splash illustration. Same rounded oversized
head, tiny torso, short limbs,2.5-3 heads tall, eyes-only face, precise contours,
crisp cel shading and smooth painted highlights at every rarity.
Increase design complexity, never realism.

**Review revision:** all six prompts now use the same subject/anatomy and
rendering clauses, not a new adventurer/adept/knight/monarch style at each form.
Wraththorn's progression is translated into swept gale-crystal spines, severe
segmented armor, divided ceremonial mantle and fractured wind coronas.
These are wind structures, not black slime thorns, a cleaver or abyss colors.
The Omnic keeps the Legendary's six principal wings, crescent-collared staff,
hovering posture and mantle, then expands them with a second wheel, two
auxiliary wings, a tall crown and double fractured coronas. It never resets
to a smaller grounded silhouette. More wings alone is not the strength test.

- Tousled silver-gray hair, teal eyes, recognizable sky-blue shoulder sash,
  navy clothing/underlayers, silver and pearl wind armor. Keep these visible.
- One wind staff, never a spear, trident or broom. Its open curved head evolves
  from a simple crook into a monumental skywheel; shaft and butt remain readable.
- Sky blue, teal, navy, silver and pearl. Opalescent facets stay within the
  cool identity; do not add orange/gold to match the key or turn him rainbow.
- Borrow the **structural progression**, not species/palette/equipment, of
  [Wraththorn](gamemodes/Delve%20into%20the%20Abyss.md) and
  [Dawnthorn](gamemodes/Soar%20to%20Heaven.md): simple body -> forged armor ->
  ceremonial mantle -> hovering winged champion -> layered wings/crown ->
  fully realized monarch with a rebuilt weapon and open orbital structures.
  Preserve the supplied starter renderer throughout.
- Owner's "evo.0" means the **beginner/base image** below. Stored gameplay/art
  indexing remains Evo.1-6; no seventh form or save-index change.

1. Copy **only the contents of one `text` block**. Do not copy a heading,
   character name, ability name, asset ID, table or this instruction.
   Names belong in game UI, not on the generated image. Prompt bodies describe
   the visual subject without a proper name, rarity label or written title.
2. Portrait blocks already include the exact evolution-matched reference.
   Icons and weapons use no `--sref` or `--sw`; do not append references.
3. Keep the supplied `--niji 6 --s 100 --q 1` and one `--no` list.
   Do not add a second exclusion list, `--style`, text captions or poster terms.

Every block explicitly ends its visual description with **plain solid orange
background (#FF8000)**: the contrasting key, not sunset scenery or lighting.
The same fill must show between limbs, wing feathers and open staff rings.
It must not tint the subject. Do not exclude orange in `--no`, which would
contradict the requested backdrop. Hex values are guidance, not pixel guarantees.
If a generated facet overlaps the key, change only the key after review.
See the [solid-color contract](cutout-background-contract.md) and
[shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md).
