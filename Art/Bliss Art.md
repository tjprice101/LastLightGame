## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu with Flora's silhouette discipline.
Same rounded oversized head, tiny torso, short limbs,2.5-3 heads tall, eyes-only
face, precise anime contours, crisp cel shading and smooth painted highlights.
Increase design complexity, never realism. Every portrait repeats identical
identity/anatomy and renderer/palette clauses.

- Ivory hair in two low swept buns with short feather-like side locks,
  pale-blue eyes, rose neck ribbon and pearl underlayers remain recognizable.
- Exactly two folding warfans, one in each hand; complete grips, hinges,
  articulated gold ribs and ivory feather panels. No bow, sword or bird.
- Ivory, pearl, soft rose, antique gold and pale blue; restrained opal facets
  within this palette, never an unrelated rainbow or copied abyss colors.
- A dramatic silhouette transformation, guided by the owner's base/final
  Wraththorn image comparison, not a small increase in decorative trim.
  Translate
  [Wraththorn](gamemodes/Delve%20into%20the%20Abyss.md): segmented plates,
  contrasting joints/fracture seams, divided mantle, separated orbit fragments,
  broken corona, branching feather wings and retained final regalia.
  armor mass, colossal equipment and sweeping regalia as well as those details.
  No slime anatomy, cleaver or aggressive horror framing.
- Bliss expresses poised stillness and precise fan motion. Gold feather ribs,
  pale-blue seams and rose fan hinges distinguish her from Aurora's solar
  lenses, Atmoso's wind staff and rose-event armor.
- Omnic retains Legendary's six principal wings, hovering duelist pose, split
  mantle and paired broad fans. Shoulder/gauntlet/greave mass changes the outer
  body contour; huge feather arrays, crown and mantle make a towering sovereign.
  Each final warfan spans twice her body height, not a small hand accessory.
  Wing counts alone never satisfy this transformation.
- All imagery is non-explicit: fully covered clothing/armor, abstract feather
  arcs in empty space, no depicted injury or bound figures. The wording review
  cannot guarantee a service moderation outcome; follow its rules and inspect
  actual outputs. See the [neutral wording contract](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md#neutral-character-wording-d-104).

  hover/fan stance and mantle. Final wings/armor/fans must be wider and denser

  gauntlets, greaves, cascading mantle and colossal fans must transform the
