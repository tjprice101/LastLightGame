## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu in clean precise anime contours,
crisp cel shading, smooth painted highlights and jewel-like color treatment.
Increase design complexity, never realism. D-109 restores the compact Bliss-style
approach: same rounded head, tiny torso, short limbs and2.5-3-head anatomy in
every form. No divine-body or geological-body transformation.

- Male earth hammer-bearer: short dark-brown swept-back hair, amber eyes,
  ochre waist sash, stone-gray underlayers and square basalt motifs throughout.
- Exactly one double-faced hammer with broad rectangular striking faces,
  thick haft, visible grip and butt. No axe, mace, cleaver or extra weapon.
- Basalt gray, warm umber, ochre, bronze, amber and restrained opal facets;
  no unrelated rainbow or copied purple slime.
- Progression changes armor coverage/mass, hammer engineering, mantle,
  wing arrays, crown and abstract earth powers, never the character's anatomy.
  Omnic retains Legendary's principal arrays, guard and mantle, expanding
  regalia and equipment rather than resetting the design.
- Keep fully covered clothing/armor, eyes-only faces, complete padded cutouts
  and non-explicit abstract motion. Neutral wording cannot guarantee service
  moderation acceptance. Follow the
  [wording contract](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md#neutral-character-wording-d-104).

outside image prose. Portrait blocks already include the exact evolution-matched
reference. Icons and weapons use no `--sref` or `--sw`; do not append references.
