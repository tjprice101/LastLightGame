# Crownfall Treasury

Phase9 currency farm:25 stages, hostile levels65-120, Luminous gemstone
crowned slimes. Six fixed creature forms correspond to Common through Omnic;
captured copies never evolve. Same portraits serve enemies and owned captures.
Standard includes only the first three forms. An owned EB banner result awards
the final Omnic form at **Lv50**, not the mission's defeated level.

Use the [shared style](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
[cutout contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Enemy portrait blocks
already include their mapped reference at `--sw 400`; items and scenery use
no `--sref` or `--sw`.
Retain rounded limbless slime anatomy, eyes-only faces, ivory-gold body,
amber core and readable gem/crown identity. Later forms become supreme and
majestic, not cute or realistic. Cutouts stay matte with hard-edged opaque
prismatic structures; scenery can use authored atmospheric lighting.

Suggested exports: cutouts960x960 PNG after background removal with full
silhouette/padding; treasury mode banner1800x600 opaque; arena1920x1080 opaque.
Preserve originals in `Art/source`. All six supplied portraits, the Treasury
header and arena are registered to the matching mode; portrait runtime IDs are
listed in `src/content/infusions.ts`. Their source hashes and reviewed color-key
settings are in `Art/root-art-intake.json`. Do not substitute another mode's
assets for future missing art.
