## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu with the requested Bliss/Bruno
compact progression: same rounded head, tiny torso, short limbs,2.5-3 heads
tall and eyes-only face in all forms. Increase design complexity, never realism.
Every portrait repeats identical identity/anatomy and renderer/palette clauses.

- Male Chaotic caster with upward plum-black hair, violet eyes, scarlet shoulder
  cord and charcoal underlayers. No anatomy/world-scale transformation.
- Open-hand psychic flame casting, **no physical handheld weapon**. Scarlet
  flames, angular violet lattice frames and upward linked emblems express
  ally empowerment, not binding enemies or healing. No extra people in cutouts.
- Plum black, deep violet, scarlet, magenta, silver and restrained amethyst/opal.
  Translate Wraththorn's segmented armor, divided mantle, broken coronas,
  branching wings and imposing regalia, not slime species or cleaver.
- First fitted plates -> substantial lattice shoulders/gauntlets -> high collar/
  mantle -> six-wing psychic court -> expanded crowned twelve-wing court.
  Omnic retains Legendary's pose, mantle, main wing pairs and casting frames,
  expanding armor and nested frame architecture, not merely adding wings.
- Empty palms stay visible through fingerless guards. Fully covered character,
  non-explicit abstract motion and upward formation emblems in empty space.
  Names/titles/labels remain outside image prose to reduce generated lettering.
- All13 blocks explicitly end with plain solid blue background `#0000FF`, flat/
  unlit through every lattice opening. Powers are opaque, hard-edged and
  non-emissive; no glow. Preserve subject colors, change only the key if needed.
- Copy only one text block. Portrait blocks already include the exact
  evolution-matched reference. Icons and focus cutouts use no `--sref` or `--sw`;
  do not append references. One `--no` list, no `--style`.
- See the [shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
  [background contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Neutral wording cannot
  guarantee service acceptance; comply with its rules and inspect actual output.

**Evolution impact (D-099), individually revised under D-110:** fixed anatomy/
body one third of4:3 canvas, epic armor/constructs around it. Final casting
frames span1.5 body heights versus Legendary's1, with three nested lattice tiers.
Six-star complexity means richer final architecture, not a fancy beginner.
