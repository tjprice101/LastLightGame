# Element medallions

Ten neutral element identifiers, not character rarity badges or star ratings.
Use the [shared renderer](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
[cutout contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Each is a framed medallion
with a contrasting opaque inset and one unmistakable centered symbol.
No lettering: names are supplied by the UI.

Emblem prompts use no `--sref` or `--sw`. Suggested key colors are starting points only:
inspect delivered colors before keying. Preserve the full frame and margin.
Light/flame symbols use opaque painted shapes, not bloom or transparent auras.
All emblems keep one readable style; they do not advance through rarity.

All ten supplied medallions are registered at
`public/assets/elements/<element-id>.png`; accessible element names remain in
the UI. Opaque RGB originals are retained under `Art/source/elements`, with
per-image keying and hashes recorded in `Art/root-art-intake.json`.
