**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu with the requested Bliss/Bruno/Disciple
compact progression. Identical identity/anatomy and renderer/palette clauses
across six forms: rounded oversized head, tiny torso, short limbs,2.5-3 heads
tall, eyes-only face. Increase design complexity, never realism.

- Short black hair/one swept side lock, lime-green eyes, mint wrist ribbons
  and charcoal underlayers remain recognizable throughout.
- Exactly two four-point shuriken with open central grips, one in each hand.
  Preserve four principal points and functional openings as nested arm plates/
  circuit collars become more ornate; no extra floating weapons.
- Charcoal, electric lime, mint, polished silver and restrained violet facets.
  No generic rainbow armor or copied slime anatomy/cleaver.
- Segmented lightning plates, charcoal joints/silver fracture seams, stepped
  shoulders/gauntlets, divided mantle, broken coronas and skeletal lightning
  wings translate Wraththorn's impact into Elise's angular star/circuit identity.
- Omnic retains Legendary's hover/throwing stance, mantle, six main wings and
  paired circuit-collared stars, then broadens armor/wing vanes, adds auxiliary
  wings/crown/double coronas and three-tier thunderwheel arms. Each final star
  spans1.5 body heights versus Legendary's1; body/anatomy remain unchanged.
- Fully covered designs and abstract electricity in empty space; no depicted
  injury or extra people. Names/titles/labels remain outside copy blocks.
- All13 blocks use plain solid orange key `#FF8000`, flat/unlit through openings,
  generous padding and no-glow hard-edged opaque powers. Preserve subject
  colors; change only the key if foreground facets overlap it.
- Copy only one text block. Portrait blocks already include the exact
  evolution-matched reference. Icons and weapons use no `--sref` or `--sw`;
  do not append references. Keep one `--no` list, no `--style`.
- See the [shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
  [background contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Wording checks cannot
  guarantee service acceptance; comply with its rules and inspect outputs.

-> first plates -> segmented armor/mantle/winglets -> hovering broken corona ->
