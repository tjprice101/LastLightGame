This naming convention is approved; proposed future art/lore/kit content does
not become implemented or mechanically approved through a name change.

## Visual escalation

**D-109 current owner direction:** restore compact character anatomy in every
form. D-107/D-108 deity-body/scale experiments are superseded. Keep the same
hair/eyes/body proportions; epicness comes from armor contour, equipment,
mantle, wings, crown and elemental geometry. Bruno restored, Bliss unchanged.

**D-099 structural escalation:** the actual portrait prompts now start with
plain starter-scale clothing and signature equipment, including the6-star lines.
Evo.2 forges the first armor/weapon; Evo.3 completes the body armor and adds
short crystal winglets. Evo.4-6 specify complete prismatic armor over torso,
shoulders, arms/hands, hips, thighs/knees, shins and feet, not just ornate shoulders.
Wing silhouettes unfold from one broad pair to six wings, then eight for5-star
finales or twelve for6-star finales. Each line's wings have its own elemental
feather geometry, rather than the same white angel wings pasted onto every unit.

Late designs explicitly wrap layered elemental ribbons, shards and open rings
around the character and increasingly monumental signature weapon/focus.
The intended impact matches the late Dawnthorn/Wraththorn slime silhouettes,
combined with the original starter evolution renderer, not realistic splash art.
Final weapons/foci have matching standalone prompts. Aurora/Disciple remain
empty-handed energy casters; ornate foci do not become held weapons.
Radiant presence is represented by saturated prismatic facets and non-emissive
painted highlights. Preserve no-glow cutouts, clear eyes/body/equipment,
complete wing tips, internal gaps and generous margins. Generated results still
need side-by-side manual review; wording cannot certify the eventual output.

## Shared generation and review

Follow the [character guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
[solid-background/no-glow contract](cutout-background-contract.md).
All character forms use oversized rounded heads, tiny torso/short limbs and
2.5-3-head-tall anatomy, body about one third of4:3 canvas. Retain
eyes-only faces, complete weapon/effects and generous margins.
Icons are readable symbols around two thirds of1:1.
Prismatic and energy details use opaque hard-edged shapes, cel shading and
non-emissive painted highlights, never source-art glow.

The starting background swatches are export aids, not character colors.
Prismatic foreground can overlap any key; inspect results, choose a different
background when needed and never erase/recolor matching subject facets. Inspect
all internal openings, weapon gaps, fan ribs, armor vanes and rings. Transparent
owner-supplied cutouts must preserve authoritative alpha at intake.

Generate and approve Evo.1 first, then compare every later form to it. A style
reference does not guarantee identity. Use approved results as visual checks;
do not invent character-reference URLs or silently change Midjourney versions.

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tools -p test_art_prompts.py` after edits.
Tests check prompt structure, form/icon counts, stars/roles, identity anchors,
ratios, prismatic escalation and shared cutout rules; output needs manual review.
