# Passion of Crimson Roses

**Status:** owner-approved event art prompt pack, Phase10 cutout revision (D-118). Prompts only; no images are
generated or registered here.

The event runs 35 stages from enemy level 80 through 140. Each Roselius form
uses one identical portrait for hostile and owned copies; captures are possible
only through level 120, and copies never evolve. Selling a copy awards material
of that form's rarity. These confirmed activity facts do not change the prompt
art or imply additional runtime behavior.

## Roselius evolution cutouts

Six fixed **female Luminous Roselius creatures**, not Element-Bearers.
The visual progression is not a captured-copy evolution mechanic.
D-118 applies Thornia's increasingly wild yet elegant rose-thorn structure
without copying her shadow palette, greatsword or character identity.

**Renderer lock:** match the compact Infernis/Tizu/Flora and Bliss/Bruno anime
contours, crisp cel shading and painted highlights. Same rounded ivory core,
crimson rose crest/two crimson eyes, rounded oversized head/tiny torso/short
covered limbs/2.5-3 heads, body one third of4:3 throughout. Eyes only: no mouth,
nose, eyebrows or other facial features. No humanoid realism/body transformation.
Crimson/antique gold/ivory stay primary; late rose-violet/sapphire-blue/warm
opal facets add chromatic resplendence without emission.

- Modest feathered angel -> first plates -> segmented rose-knight armor ->
  six-wing seraph -> eight-wing sovereign -> sixteen-wing eternal rose court.
  Armor mass/seams/pauldrons/gauntlets, divided mantle, crown and thorn structures
  strengthen even ignoring wing count. No held weapons.
- Right-facing open-palm casting; later airborne twist/raised knee. Omnic
  retains Legendary's main eight wings, pose/mantle/collar and three tilted
  rose-rings, then adds auxiliary wings, branching armor/crown/pennants, fourth
  ring and expanded offset thorn fans/interwoven arches/ribbons/rose fragments.
- Whole ensemble spans50/60/72/84/94/96% width/height across six forms, with
 25/20/14/8/3/2% per-side key margins. Late structures reach toward all sides/
  corners; face/feet/armor remain readable, all tips inside margins.
  These are prompt targets, not measured image coverage.
- All12 revised cutouts use flat unlit green `#00FF00` through openings,
  hard-edged opaque powers, painted non-emissive facets and lettering exclusions.
  No glow/scenery. Preserve subject colors; adjust only key if review finds overlap.
  Proper names/rarity labels stay in headings, not copy-ready image prose.
- Enemy portrait blocks already include their mapped reference at `--sw 400`.
  Materials, banners, arenas and all non-portrait art use no `--sref` or `--sw`.
  One `--no`, no `--style`. See the [shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md)
  and [background contract](cutout-background-contract.md).

**Scope:** six creature portraits/six materials only. Header/arena/summoning
artpiece, runtime IDs, captures/drops/sales/costs and other packs unchanged.
**Pause for Roselius/material review before another line.**

## Seed of Rosethorn material chain

Six face-free collectibles, same compact anime/cel renderer and rose-gold
identity, not creatures or weapon drops. Keep each complete object plus its
ornamentation at roughly two thirds of its square canvas with generous padding
and small-icon readability. Do not apply creature near-full-canvas framing.

Seed's simple gold husk -> closed Bud/sepals -> open Bloom/calyx -> framed
heraldic Crest -> armored rose-Heart/rings -> fully unfolded rose-Soul.
Geometry grows wilder yet elegant through layered petals, tilted rose-rings,
thorn fans and ribbon sweeps, not dense indistinguishable noise. Soul retains
Heart's faceted core/petal armor/gold thorns/two rings/six ribbons, expanding
with triple-tier petals/crown/third ring/three extra ribbons. Late chromatic
facets complement the crimson/gold/ivory core. Named six material headings
still map to the authored drop/sale/cost chain; no extra material tier.
