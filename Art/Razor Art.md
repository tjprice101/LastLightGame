## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu with the requested compact
Bliss/Bruno/Disciple/Elise progression. Identical identity/anatomy and renderer/
palette clauses across six forms: rounded oversized head, tiny torso, short
limbs,2.5-3 heads tall, eyes-only face. Increase design complexity, never realism.

- Cropped ink-black hair, one silver forelock, violet eyes, black neck scarf
  and graphite underlayers remain recognizable throughout.
- One broad sword of pure night, opaque ink-black blade with silver-violet edge,
  never a metal katana. Crescent-bastion guards, amethyst clasps and stepped
  ridges become increasingly defined; no second weapon or held shield.
- Ink black/graphite/deep violet/silver, restrained amethyst-opal facets.
  No generic rainbow suit or copied slime anatomy/cleaver.
- Tank identity grows through segmented obsidian armor, graphite joints/silver
  fracture seams, tower pauldrons, substantial gauntlets/greaves, divided
  mantle and crescent shield vanes. Floating eclipse ramparts are abstract
  powers in empty space, not scenery or additional equipment.
- Omnic retains Legendary's braced elevated guard, royal mantle, six principal
  wings, double crescent guard/three hilt clasps and eclipse ramparts. Broaden
  armor/vanes, add auxiliary wings/crown/double coronas and five blade ridges.
  Blade span1.5 ->2 body heights; unchanged body size/anatomy throughout.
- Fully covered designs, no depicted injury or extra people. Names, rarity/
  star labels and IDs remain outside copy blocks.
- All13 blocks use plain solid green key `#00FF00`, flat/unlit through openings,
  generous padding and hard-edged opaque no-glow powers. Preserve subject
  colors; change only the key if foreground facets overlap it.
- Copy one text block. Portrait blocks already include the exact
  evolution-matched reference. Icons and weapons use no `--sref` or `--sw`;
  do not append references. Keep one `--no` list, no `--style`.
- See the [shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
  [background contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Wording checks cannot
  guarantee service acceptance; comply with its rules and inspect outputs.

**Evolution impact (D-099), individually revised under D-112:** plain tunic/
sword -> first plates -> segmented bastion armor/mantle/winglets -> high collar/
broken corona -> six-wing eclipse bastion -> expanded twelve-wing night fortress.
Wraththorn-like impact comes from equipment/armor architecture, not different
anatomy. Same compact body one third of4:3 canvas; regalia grows around it.
Six forms, fixed6-star identity; icon symbols do not establish combat mechanics.
