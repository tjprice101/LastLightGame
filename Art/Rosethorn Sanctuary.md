# Rosethorn Sanctuary

Phase10 Null-Prismatica farm:25 stages, hostile levels65-120, six fixed Tranquilitic
Rosethorn Wisp forms from Common to Omnic. Captured copies never evolve.
Use identical portraits for hostile and owned creatures, not separate styles.
Standard includes only forms1-3; forms4-6 come from mission captures.

Follow the [shared style](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
[cutout contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Enemy portrait blocks
already include their mapped reference at `--sw 400`; all non-portrait art
uses no `--sref` or `--sw`.
These are floating, limbless flame-shaped spirits, not slimes, humans or angels.
Keep an ivory core, rose-pink/red matte flame lobes, antique-gold thorn/laurel
regalia and two eyes only. Flame shapes are opaque non-emissive ribbons with
painted highlights, never fire glow, bloom, transparent smoke or particles.
Higher forms become divine, regal and transcendent without changing the compact
anime/cel renderer or obscuring the original rosefire core.

Suggested exports: cutouts960x960 PNG after background removal, activity
header1800x600 opaque PNG, arena1920x1080 opaque PNG. Preserve full silhouette,
background-color gaps and generous padding; preserve originals under Art/source.
All six supplied portraits, the Sanctuary header and arena are registered to
the matching mode. Portrait runtime IDs are listed in `src/content/infusions.ts`;
source hashes and reviewed color-key settings are in `Art/root-art-intake.json`.
Do not request missing PNGs or substitute another mode's assets.
