## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu's clean anime contours, crisp cel
shading, smooth non-emissive painted highlights and jewel-like colors. D-114
restores the Bliss/Bruno-style compact anatomy in every form: rounded oversized
head, tiny torso, short limbs,2.5-3 heads tall and body one third of4:3 canvas.
The rejected D-113 elongated proportions/larger-body framing are superseded.
Splendor grows through armor mass, mantle, bow engineering, wings and elemental
architecture around the same body, never realism, mutation or splash scenery.

- Deep-crimson bob/ivory rose clip/crimson eyes remain fixed; eyes-only face.
  Deep crimson, antique gold, pearlescent ivory and rose facets throughout.
  D-115 adds rose-violet, sapphire-blue and warm opal chromatic facets to late
  armor/wings/bow/rings, enriching rather than replacing the crimson-gold core.
  Painted facet contrast creates resplendence; no emitted light or green spill.
- One functional longbow, visible grip/string and one nocked arrow. Later
  airborne twisting draw keeps bow/arrow beside the face, never across eyes.
  No extra weapons/people or depicted injury; clothing/armor fully covered.
- Segmented crimson enamel/gold fracture seams/ivory joints grow into monumental
  rose-canopy pauldrons, gauntlets/greaves, petal mantle and celestial regalia.
  Epic4 ->Legendary8 ->Omnic16 wings, paired and separated, not a featureless mass.
- Omnic retains Legendary's eight main wings, airborne draw/raised knee,
  high collar, royal mantle and thorn-arched rose-lens bow. Broader branching
  armor/vanes, auxiliary wings, nine-petal crown/floating diadem and three
  orbital sun-rings create cosmic authority around the same compact archer.
  Gold thorn fans/open arches, increasing rose fragments and sweeping petal
  ribbons amplify the effects while preserving clear gaps and readable eyes.
  Bow span1.5 ->2 body heights; triple arches/nested lens amplify its engineering.
- Cosmic presence uses isolated hard-edged stars/rings/roses, never scenery,
  emission or realistic anatomy. Powers stay opaque, saturated and crisp.
- D-120 Omnic emphasizes **interior density**, not just outer span: a very
  busy rear tapestry of overlapping rose mandalas, branching thorn lattice,
  radial petal rays, counter-sweeping ribbons and clustered prism fragments
  occupies nearly all space behind the wings/body. No large empty interior
  patches or isolated central figure; narrow key channels and a clear face/
  bow window retain readability. These are opaque elemental constructs, not
  landscape scenery, soft lighting or a textured key background.
- D-116 complete artwork spreads progressively: Common50%, Uncommon60%,
  Rare72%, Epic84%, Legendary94%, Omnic96% of canvas width and height.
  Corresponding per-side key margins25/20/14/8/3/2%. These measure the whole
  ensemble, not painted pixel coverage or body height. Legendary/Omnic spread
  wings, mantle, thorn arches and ribbons toward every side and corner;
  no isolated central cluster with a few outlying sparks. Nearly edge-to-edge
  composition, not a cropped/full-bleed scene or larger body. Compact anatomy
  and body one third remain; wings, mantle and effects fill the surrounding
  space with clear background channels and visible face/weapon.
- All13 blocks: flat unlit green `#00FF00` through openings, complete padded
  silhouettes, no glow, expanded text exclusions. Names/titles outside prose.
  Keep subject colors; change the key only if review shows foreground overlap.
- Copy one block. Portrait blocks already include the exact evolution-matched
  reference. Icons and weapons use no `--sref` or `--sw`; do not append references.
  One `--no`, no `--style`. See the [shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md)
  and [background contract](cutout-background-contract.md).
  Local wording checks cannot guarantee moderation acceptance.
