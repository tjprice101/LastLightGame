# Summoning banner art

## Owner-approved contract

Summoning banners are full-bleed **16:9 artpieces** with **Omnic-tier visual
ambition**, embodying the banner's overall identity. They are not portrait grids,
cropped dungeon headers or cards for individual possible rewards.
Omnic here describes art direction, not guaranteed acquisition rarity.
The drop-rate table remains text-only: name, actual awarded rarity/star value,
and probability. Do not bake rates, labels, UI or lettering into the art.

Keep the [shared renderer](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md):
clean anime contours, crisp cel shading, smooth painted highlights and jewel-like
colors. Escalate to transcendent architecture, fully realized elemental
structures and majestic prismatic composition, not realism or cute wording.
Scenery is opaque and full-bleed; cutout key backgrounds do not apply.
If characters are later commissioned into a banner, keep their established
compact anatomy, eyes-only faces and recognizable identity.

Append the actual approved `--sref <approved_reference_image_url> --sw 200`
for scenery. Replace the placeholder with the established approved reference;
do not invent a URL. Review generated artwork against supplied art before use.

## Standard Banner / Omnic elemental convergence

Identity: Last Light's general elemental summoning sanctuary. Unite Infernis's
fire/greatsword, Tizu's water/spear and Flora's nature/bow motifs with Heaven's
serene jade-ivory architecture and Abyss's formidable plum-obsidian thorn forms.
Express these as architectural emblems, not pictures of individual outcomes.
Include Crownfall Treasury's amber gemstone/crown architecture now that its
content exists, and Rosethorn Sanctuary's divine ivory/rosefire thorn arches
and laurel architecture. No invented reward creatures or promised six-star EBs.

Suggested export: **1920x1080 PNG**, opaque, sRGB.
Preserve approved original under `Art/source/banners`.
Intended runtime path: `public/assets/banners/summon-standard.png`.
The supplied Standard banner is registered through
`standardBanner.artwork.path` as `banners/summon-standard.png`. Its opaque
original is retained under `Art/source/banners`; intake provenance is in
`Art/root-art-intake.json`. Future banners still remain neutral until supplied
and reviewed; never make missing-image requests or borrow unrelated art.
