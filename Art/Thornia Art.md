## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu and the compact Bliss/Bruno direction:
rounded oversized head, tiny torso, short limbs,2.5-3 heads tall, body one third
of4:3 canvas throughout. Identical identity/anatomy/renderer/palette clauses in
every portrait. Eyes only: no mouth/nose/eyebrows/other facial marks.
Design complexity increases, never realism, elongated anatomy or monstrous bodies.

- Dark hair/crimson rose clasp/narrow crimson eyes stay recognizable.
  Charcoal/deep crimson/antique gold/pearlescent ivory remain primary; later
  amethyst/rose-violet/warm opal facets enrich her shadow identity.
- Exactly one greatsword with readable grip/guard/complete blade. Simple steel
  develops an obsidian blade, gold thorn spine, nested rose guard/ivory bevel.
  Blade spans1 body height Rare,1.25 Epic,1.5 Legendary,2 Omnic.
- Segmented armor/gold fracture seams/ivory joints, massive thorn shoulders,
  gauntlets/greaves, divided mantle, rose crown and4/8/16 late wings carry
  formidable tank identity. No extra held shield or weapon.
- Late action is an airborne twisting sword sweep with raised knee, blade
  beside the body/face. Omnic retains Legendary's main eight wings/pose/
  royal mantle/high collar/sword foundation, expanding every layer.
- Increasingly wild yet elegant shadow-garden geometry, not disorder that
  hides the subject: tilted broken eclipse rings, off-axis spiral thorn fans,
  interwoven open arches, sweeping crimson ribbons and ivory rose fragments.
  One ring/four ribbons Epic ->two/six Legendary ->three/nine Omnic.
  Opposing sweeps balance the composition; no scenery or depicted injury.
- Whole ensemble spread50/60/72/84/94/96% width/height, margins25/20/14/8/3/2%.
  Late wings/mantle/thorn arches/ribbons reach toward all sides/corners, never
  touch edges. Percentages are generation targets, not measured assets or
  painted-pixel coverage. Keep face/blade/open channels readable.
- All13 green keys `#00FF00` are flat/unlit through openings. Powers opaque
  hard-edged/non-emissive; chromatic facets are painted, never source glow.
  Preserve subject palette; change only the key if foreground overlap occurs.
- Names/titles outside copy prose, one `--no`, no `--style`.
  Portrait blocks already include the exact evolution-matched reference.
  Icons and weapons use no `--sref` or `--sw`; do not append references.
  See [shared guide](midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) and
  [background contract](cutout-background-contract.md). Wording checks cannot
  guarantee moderation acceptance; inspect generated output.
