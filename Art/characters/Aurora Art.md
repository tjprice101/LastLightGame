## Identity and generation contract

**Renderer lock:** match Infernis and Tizu, with Flora's silhouette discipline.
Same tiny body, rounded oversized head, short limbs,2.5-3 heads tall, eyes-only
face, clean anime contours, crisp cel shading and smooth painted highlights.
Increase design complexity, never realism. All six portrait blocks use the
same identity/anatomy and renderer/palette clauses; rarity never changes style.

- Pearl-white chin-length bob with two short side locks, amber eyes, ivory neck
  ribbon, obsidian underlayers. Hair, eyes and ribbon remain readable.
- Open-hand light-energy casting, **no physical handheld weapon**. Open lens
  circles, amber geometric bands and obsidian eclipse notches express enemy
  suppression, not healing, shield equipment or a summoned creature.
- Ivory, pearl, amber, antique gold and obsidian; restrained opal facets within
  that identity. Do not copy Atmoso's wind palette, Wraththorn's purple/pink
  slime or Rosetta's crimson roses and bow.
- Translate [Wraththorn's](../creatures/gamemodes/Delve%20into%20the%20Abyss.md) structural
  progression: first plates -> segmented ceremonial armor/mantle/fragments ->
  hovering broken corona -> six-wing severe regalia -> crowned expanded final
  architecture. [Dawnthorn](../creatures/gamemodes/Soar%20to%20Heaven.md) supports the
  strong ivory/black/gold separation. Neither supplies her anatomy or weapon.
- Her unique language is **lens-shaped solar feathers, eclipse seams and
  geometric bands**. No copied slime thorns or cleaver. Late wings
  are branching prism blades, not generic soft bird feathers.
- Six-star final complexity is additional structure, not a fancy beginner.
  Omnic preserves Legendary's six main wings, royal mantle, hovering palms and
  crescent-collared lenses. It broadens those structures and adds six smaller
  auxiliary wings, layered crown and double fractured coronas.
