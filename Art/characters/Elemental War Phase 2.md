## Generation and identity contract

The supplied pixels, not the old Moon filename or old prose, own these four appearances. All object prompts borrow the actual equipment, costume relief and colored energy. Do not copy blue-purple Moon phases onto these War designs or reduce them to black and white. There are no new portrait requests, no fabricated portrait reference URLs, no hidden model switch and no claimed one-image initial-generation modifier. Objects/scenery are reference-free. Keep the original compact anime/cel renderer, niji6, s100, q1 and appropriate aspect ratios. Test one ability and one Master per owner before a full batch; Draft only when supported by the selected model, upscale accepted sources only when needed. Cutout powers are opaque and sharp, with complete 15% safety margins; bloom belongs to scenery/runtime, not keyed edges.

Every Master is an immense owner-shaped mechanical sculpture with gears, axles, chambers or pistons, not a generic monochrome chassis, building or miniature wearer. Shared Normal Attack/Defense/Burn/Weaken artwork is reused, not requested again. Status clocks/numbers stay HTML.
