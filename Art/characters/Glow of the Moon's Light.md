## Vespera visual-language pilot

All16 Vespera prompts use a shorter subject-first composition: fixed identity and pose, approved stage costume/powers, renderer, palette, then containment/key and parameters. Six portraits, four abilities, one optional weapon, three Masters and two status icons are covered. Full approved design/change notes remain beside portraits; no appearance or kit changes. Other characters and shared event scenery are not part of this pilot.

Generate Common and Omnic plus one ability icon first; inspect identity, single standard/pennant, flame origins, silhouette clearance and crisp rendering before spending on the remaining set. No personalization or Omni Reference is silently enabled. Upscale only accepted sources needing more resolution. Prompt checks validate written constraints, not generated pixels or guaranteed results.

## Identity, complexity and reference contract

All36 portraits preserve exact fixed features and approved stage rows. Adjacent change notes are intentional costume/power evolution, never hair/eye/skin transformations. Common/Uncommon remain simple even for6-stars. Legendary architecture grows into Omnic; wing counts/materials differ by character. Titles are display prefixes outside image prose, not banner names, combat mode names or written lettering.

Same original Infernis/Tizu compact anime/cel renderer, natural authored skin tones and strict black-white-silver costume/equipment/power palette. Eyes are the only anatomical facial features. Entire ensemble stays inside70% with15% uninterrupted key margin on every edge; no cropped tips, haze, soft bloom or glow on edges. Painted metallic reflections remain.

Portraits alone use the guide's exact evolution-matched signed URLs at150. They are preserved verbatim, not verified perpetually available; if expired, stop generation and obtain renewed owner URLs. Icons/equipment/Masters/scenery use no reference flags. Scenery is full-bleed and may have moonlight glare/bloom; no scenery key/background removal.
