# Calamitic Conduits

Six reference-free, copy-ready square icon prompts for five new Calamitic
Conduits and their shared Calamitic Tokens icon. The five exact runtime IDs
below now resolve installed artwork (D-325); these friendly generation labels
remain historical prompt filenames, not runtime URLs. The delivered token is
singular `Calamitic Token.png`, archived without renaming. See
[reviewed intake](../../docs/art-workflow.md#delivered-calamitic-artwork-d-325).
These designs are Rise's own prized
annihilation machinery, not prototypes made collaboratively with Let. Calamitic
is above Omnic in art tier and Universal in affinity; it is not a new element.
No gameplay effect is specified here.

## Shared style lock

One standalone object per high-resolution PNG, square 1:1, isolated like a
premium collectible item icon. Use the established compact anime/cel gacha
collectible-item renderer: crisp clean contours, controlled cel shading,
painted dimensional metal facets and non-emissive highlights. Calamitic
machinery is overwhelmingly black and white metal, with intensely vibrant
crimson accents and opaque red-and-white chaotic energy. Its extraordinary
cosmic scale and engineering complexity come from intricate nested mechanical
layers, not scenery or extra objects. Keep dense interior construction while
leaving narrow key gaps between readable parts. Enclose the entire machine,
every ring, rail, arm, channel, flame and fragment inside a continuous empty
margin of at least 15% on every side and corner; pull the complete composition
back uniformly, never crop or simplify it. Powers are opaque crisp ribbons,
flame tongues, facets and shapes with painted non-emissive highlights only:
no generated glow, emission, haze, translucency or background spill. Use a
perfectly flat unlit pure-green `#00FF00` key background through every opening;
the subject palette lock never applies to that background. No `--sref` or
`--sw`. Every prompt below stands alone and can be copied independently.
