## Shared production contract

Thornia D-117 and Crinso D-119 are the new escalation standard: compact bodies,
clean precise anime contours, crisp cel shading, smooth non-emissive painted
highlights, jewel-like colors, increasingly wild but elegant opposing sweeps,
open arches, wing vanes and prismatic mechanical regalia. No realistic anatomy,
gritty rendering or elongated humans. Machines are creatures, not Element-Bearers;
the six designs are distinct encounters, not capturable evolution copies.
Early enemies are broken black/white futuristic machines; later repairs unfold
into majestic mythological mechanical creations. The central white engine is
shared world scenery, not a seventh enemy.

Cutouts: opaque hard-edged powers, no source glow, flat green through gaps,
no scenery, all tips inside margins. Face-free Conduits occupy two thirds of
square canvases and remain readable at icon size. Enemy bodies stay compact
inside4:3. D-154 replaces near-edge targets: ensemble spread is at most
50/60/70/70/70/70%, with at least25/20/15/15/15/15% per-side margins.
Measure the entire artwork, including its outermost wing/ribbon/fragment,
not just the body. Keep dense elaborate interiors; uniformly pull back rather
than deleting wings, equipment or effects. These are generation targets
requiring full-resolution visual inspection, not guaranteed output dimensions.
Scenery is full-bleed with lighting; the no-glow restriction does not apply.

Enemy portrait blocks use no `--sref` or `--sw` under D-155.
Authored creature colors and written compact rendering/contour/cel-shading
instructions control the design without an image reference. Early machines
stay monochrome, Kirin stays ivory/silver/pearl/pale rose, Leviathan stays
sapphire/cobalt/ivory/pearl/opal, Phoenix stays crimson-led, and Ouroboros keeps
its authored prismatic palette. Do not forbid colors intentionally in a design.
Character prompts retain their references; all enemy packs are reference-free.
Items, Conduits, banners and arenas use no `--sref` or `--sw`.
See [shared guide](../guides/midjourney-character-style-prompt.md)
and [background contract](../guides/cutout-background-contract.md).

Intake: archive originals under `Art/source/machines`, review keys and facing
offline, then export icons to `public/assets/conduits/<id>.png`, enemy sprites
to `public/assets/enemies/<id>.png`, header to
`public/assets/banners/machines-banner.png`, arena to
`public/assets/backgrounds/machines-arena.png`. Supplied alpha is authoritative.
Register only delivered/reviewed assets; never request missing PNGs at runtime.
Current originals/provenance: [machines-art-intake.json](../provenance/machines-art-intake.json).
Reproduce with `python tools\intake_machine_art.py --apply` from the repository
root; see [intake workflow](../../docs/art-workflow.md#machine-expansion-art-d-124d-128).
