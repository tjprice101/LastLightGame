# Black Diamonds Against The Light — Event & Creature Prompts

**Status:** Generation-ready art prompt pack for the "Black Diamonds Against The Light" special event and banner. Prompts only; no images are generated or registered here.

The event runs 35 stages from enemy level 80 through 140. Each Kharonite form uses one identical portrait for hostile and owned copies; captures are possible only through level 120, and copies never evolve. Selling a copy awards material of that form's rarity.

## Kharonite evolution cutouts

Six fixed **crystalline Black Diamond creatures**, Kharonite, not Element-Bearers.

**Renderer lock:** match the compact Infernis/Tizu/Flora and Bliss/Bruno anime contours, crisp cel shading and painted highlights. Rounded dark diamond core, two clear crimson eyes only with no other facial features, rounded oversized head, tiny torso, short covered limbs, 2.5 to 3 heads tall, body one third of 4:3 canvas throughout. Eyes only: no mouth, nose, eyebrows or other facial features. No humanoid realism or body transformation. Black, stark white, charcoal, and crimson stay primary; elemental diamond facets add chromatic refraction without emission.

- Modest floating crystal entity -> fitted plates -> segmented diamond-knight armor -> six-wing colossus -> eight-wing monarch -> sixteen-wing sovereign of the diamond court. Armor mass, seams, pauldrons, gauntlets, mantle, crown and diamond geometries strengthen even ignoring wing count. No held weapons.
- Right-facing open-palm casting stance.
- Whole ensemble spans 50/60/72/84/94/96% width/height across six forms, with 25/20/14/8/3/2% per-side key margins. Late structures reach toward all sides and corners; face, feet, and armor remain readable, all tips inside margins.
- All 12 cutouts use flat unlit green `#00FF00` through openings, hard-edged opaque powers, painted non-emissive facets and lettering exclusions. No glow or scenery.
- Enemy portrait blocks use no `--sref` or `--sw` under D-155. Materials, banners, arenas and all non-portrait art use no `--sref` or `--sw`.
