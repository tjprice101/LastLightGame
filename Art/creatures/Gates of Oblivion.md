## Shared production contract

Ten original Rise constructs (D-435), one form each, Lv.125-170 across 25 floors. Every construct shares the Rise palette - black and white metal, pearlescent ivory enamel and deep crimson seams - plus one accent for its combat element. Compact chibi creature rendering: rounded oversized heads, two crimson sensor eyes, no other facial features. Designs escalate from a small acolyte to the colossal Gatekeeper.

Cutouts: completely solid flat green `#00FF00` background with no gradient, floor or shadow; opaque hard-edged powers, no glow. Edge clearance follows D-135/D-154: the whole ensemble spans at most 50/55/60/65/70% of the canvas with at least 25/22/20/17/15% empty margin on every side, measured from the outermost tip. Later constructs grow denser inside that footprint, not wider. Enemy prompts use no `--sref`/`--sw` (D-155); header and arena are full-bleed scenery.

Intake: archive originals under `Art/source/oblivion`, review keys and facing offline, export enemy sprites to `public/assets/enemies/<art id>.png`, the header to `public/assets/banners/oblivion-banner.png` and the arena to `public/assets/backgrounds/oblivion-arena.png`. Supplied alpha is authoritative. Register only delivered, reviewed assets; never request missing PNGs at runtime.
