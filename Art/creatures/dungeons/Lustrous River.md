# Lustrous River - Luminous Art Pack

**Cutout background rule:** [Solid-color contract](../../guides/cutout-background-contract.md). Subject identity, design and elemental colors come first. End the prompt with a short plain solid, unlit background instruction; No glows or glowing visual effects: use opaque solid-color elemental lines, ribbons, rings and shapes with crisp edges; painted highlights are non-emissive. Never recolor the subject to suit the background. Arenas and banners remain scenery.

**Element:** Luminous (Light). Follow the [generation contract](README.md),
with reference-free enemy prompts under D-155. Eight distinct enemies, not
evolution forms or Heavens infusion wisps. Light uses gold, opal and vivid non-emissive prisms.

**Escalation:** modest lantern/glass gear grows into commanding opal shell
facets, formidable crystalline wings, majestic solar arrays and the fully
unfolded sevenfold mirror sovereign with bounded prismatic ray spirals.
Materials progress from a simple seed to articulated sun-ray cradles and a
supreme light emblem. Keep the same compact renderer and painted, not glowing,
source-art highlights.
See [pending-art direction](README.md#pending-art-rarity-escalation-pass).

## Monster lineup and proposed drops

All eight enemies, six material cutouts, banner and arena are supplied and wired.
Originals are preserved under `Art/source`; reviewed export settings/provenance
are in [the intake manifest](../../provenance/lustrous-river-intake.json).
Battle facing uses per-image gaze/weapon metadata: left-authored Guardian,
Griffin, Oracle and Kirin sprites mirror right; the other four remain unchanged.
Only images mirror, never labels or cut-in panels.
The suggested bands
and pools below are art proposals, not the [runtime encounter/drop rules](../../../docs/gameplay-and-elements.md).
Higher rarities remain less likely, with low-rarity drops still possible late.
