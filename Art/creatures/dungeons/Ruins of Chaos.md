# Ruins of Chaos - Chaotic Art Pack

**Cutout background rule:** [Solid-color contract](../../guides/cutout-background-contract.md). Subject identity, design and elemental colors come first. End the prompt with a short plain solid, unlit background instruction; No glows or glowing visual effects: use opaque solid-color elemental lines, ribbons, rings and shapes with crisp edges; painted highlights are non-emissive. Never recolor the subject to suit the background. Arenas and banners remain scenery.

**Element:** Chaotic (Dark Matter/Energy). Follow the
[generation contract](README.md), with reference-free enemy prompts under D-155.
Eight distinct enemies, not evolution forms or royal Abyss infusion wisps.
Chaos uses impossible geometry, indigo-magenta matter and warped crystal orbits.
Keep creatures stylized and readable; later designs become formidable and majestic, never realistic body horror.

**Escalation:** simple floating cubes and shard tools grow into commanding
offset prism plates, formidable polygon sails, majestic impossible crowns and
the fully unfolded reality-bending sovereign lattice with nested prismatic
matter spirals. Materials progress from a simple seed to articulated offset
cradles and a supreme chaos emblem. Same compact renderer, increasingly
extraordinary geometry rather than babyish language or realistic horror.
See [pending-art direction](README.md#pending-art-rarity-escalation-pass).
