# Valley of Solitude - Ominous Art Pack

**Cutout background rule:** [Solid-color contract](../../guides/cutout-background-contract.md). Subject identity, design and elemental colors come first. End the prompt with a short plain solid, unlit background instruction; No glows or glowing visual effects: use opaque solid-color elemental lines, ribbons, rings and shapes with crisp edges; painted highlights are non-emissive. Never recolor the subject to suit the background. Arenas and banners remain scenery.

**Element:** Ominous (Shadow). Follow the [generation contract](README.md),
with reference-free enemy prompts under D-155. Eight distinct enemies, not
evolution forms or Abyss infusion wisps. Shadow uses velvet silhouettes, violet
reflections and silver accents rather than gore or realistic horror.

**Escalation:** restrained twig/veil gear grows into commanding lunar blades,
formidable silver-thread armor, majestic antlers and the fully unfolded eclipse
monarch with nested prismatic velvet spirals. Materials progress from a simple
seed to articulated lunar cradles and a supreme shadow emblem. Later creatures
are awe-inspiring, not cute; compact anatomy and readable eyes-only faces remain.
See [pending-art direction](README.md#pending-art-rarity-escalation-pass).
