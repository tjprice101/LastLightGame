# Soar to Heaven - Dawnthorn Slime Line

**Cutout background rule:** [Solid-color contract](../../guides/cutout-background-contract.md). Subject identity, design and elemental colors come first. End the prompt with a short plain solid, unlit background instruction; No glows or glowing visual effects: use opaque solid-color elemental lines, ribbons, rings and shapes with crisp edges; painted highlights are non-emissive. Never recolor the subject to suit the background. Arenas and banners remain scenery.

Follow the [shared mode contract](README.md) for generation, export, elemental
ownership and live35-stage material bands. Six total forms share one white
slime identity, with vibrant red energy clashing against metallic golden thorns
and crisp black inlays. **Mood:** exalted, severe, formidable celestial judgment;
not gentle cloud creatures. Increasing detail follows the character evolution
trees without copying their weapons or replacing the slime with a human.

**Permanent identity:** Dawnthorn Slime. Every later form retains this exact
name after its evolution title: **"<title>, Dawnthorn Slime"**. The title denotes
its stronger stage/level form, not a different enemy or species. Every generation
prompt below depicts that same named slime; armor and regalia grow around its
recognizable white gelatinous core, scarlet eyes and red-gold thorns.
