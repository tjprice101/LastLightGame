# Precipice of the Earth - Tectonic Art Pack

**Cutout background rule:** [Solid-color contract](../cutout-background-contract.md). Subject identity, design and elemental colors come first. End the prompt with a short plain solid, unlit background instruction; No glows or glowing visual effects: use opaque solid-color elemental lines, ribbons, rings and shapes with crisp edges; painted highlights are non-emissive. Never recolor the subject to suit the background. Arenas and banners remain scenery.

**Element:** Tectonic (Earth). Follow the [generation contract](README.md),
including the approved style-reference suffix. Eight distinct enemies, not
evolution forms. Earth emphasizes weight, geodes and carved mineral armor.

**Escalation:** restrained pebble/scavenger gear grows into commanding flint
plates, formidable split-geode armor, majestic terraced mineral equipment and
the final mountain-spire sovereign with nested prismatic fault rings. Materials
progress from a simple seed to articulated summit cradles and a supreme earth
emblem. Same compact renderer; later creatures are not described as cute.
See [pending-art direction](README.md#pending-art-rarity-escalation-pass).

## Monster lineup and proposed drops

All eight enemy portraits, six materials, banner and arena are supplied and wired.
Originals are preserved in `Art/source`; [intake provenance](../precipice-earth-intake.json)
records reviewed keys, enclosed-hole seeds and protected crystal regions.
The incoming `Flntback Armadiillo.png` spelling remains in the source archive;
runtime naming is Flintback Armadillo. Directional enemy portraits mirror right
in battle; genuinely frontal Atlas/Behemoth remain unchanged.
The suggested bands
and pools below are art proposals, not the [runtime encounter/drop rules](../../docs/gameplay-and-elements.md).
Higher rarities remain less likely, with low-rarity drops still possible late.
