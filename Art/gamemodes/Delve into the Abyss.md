# Delve into the Abyss - Wraththorn Slime Line

**Cutout background rule:** [Solid-color contract](../cutout-background-contract.md). Subject identity, design and elemental colors come first. End the prompt with a short plain solid, unlit background instruction; No glows or glowing visual effects: use opaque solid-color elemental lines, ribbons, rings and shapes with crisp edges; painted highlights are non-emissive. Never recolor the subject to suit the background. Arenas and banners remain scenery.

Follow the [shared mode contract](README.md) for generation, export, elemental
ownership and the live35-stage material bands. Six total forms share one
black slime identity, with deep cosmic purple fading into hot pink at thorn tips.
White is a harsh fractured accent against black, not a soft pastel glow.
**Mood:** oppressive, regal, furious; beautiful cosmic ruin with dangerous edges.
More elaborate than ordinary dungeon enemies, still unmistakably Last Light chibi.

**Permanent identity:** Wraththorn Slime. Every later form retains this exact
name after its evolution title: **"<title>, Wraththorn Slime"**. The title denotes
its stronger stage/level form, not a different enemy or species. Every generation
prompt below depicts that same named slime; armor and regalia grow around its
recognizable black gelatinous core, pink eyes and purple-pink thorns.
