# Calamitic Materials

Ten reference-free face-free items. Seed to Soul use Common to Omnic; Fragment of Rise is Calamitic, above Soul. Feather and Splinter belong to Soar/Delve; Shard of Calamity is the Ascension material; no unapproved rarity assigned to these specialties.

**All Phase 0 images in this pack were delivered and installed (D-441).** Originals, actual runtime mappings and reviewed keys/gaps/hashes are preserved by tools/intake_calamity_phase0_art.py and its provenance manifest. The proposed filenames and prompts below are generation history, not new commissions. Names, titles, rarity and mechanics are metadata outside image prose. Black/white primary and secondary colors, sharp saturated sparkling crimson accents; pearlescent prismatic sheen means bounded faceting, never extra hues. The green key is exempt from subject palette exclusions. Only portraits use the exact evolution-matched character references at --sw 400 for this owner-requested pack; historical packs remain unchanged.
