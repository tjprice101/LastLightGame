# Archive gallery banners

Three related headers for the unified Archives submenu. Use the
[shared style](../guides/midjourney-character-style-prompt.md) without `--sref` or `--sw`.
Keep one architectural visual family, with distinct gallery themes.
These are full-bleed scenery, not cutouts. No baked-in words: UI overlays titles.
Reserve the left/central third for readable text.

The three supplied scenery banners are registered at
`public/assets/banners/archive-characters.png`,
`public/assets/banners/archive-conduits.png` and
`public/assets/banners/archive-creatures.png`. They remain opaque full-bleed
art; originals are retained under `Art/source/banners` and listed in
`Art/provenance/root-art-intake.json`.
