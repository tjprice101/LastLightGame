# Native identity status symbols

**27 new pictograms**, not new ability portraits or UI screenshots.
These cover the D-316 native states that currently use accurate live text.
Existing character-specific passive/Skill1/Skill2/Last Flare images remain
installed; all Normal/Defense actions already use the two universal images.
Do not regenerate64 ability images just because mechanics changed.

## Delivery and mapping

Each filename below is a proposed intake filename, not a registered asset URL.
Supply square PNG originals. Exports can later become256px icons after review.
Use the owner's character palettes, not a universal rainbow status palette.
Keep counts, recipient/owner, turn/phase expiry, blade location and enemy-skill
forecast as accessible HTML. The symbol never replaces those exact values.
One image represents a state; do not generate one image per stack/recipient.

## Trophy-style symbol backing

Every mechanic symbol sits prominently in front of an ornamental trophy-like
crest backing, raised above a short stepped award pedestal. The backing is
part of the cutout object, not scenery or a rectangular image background.
Use a consistent upright award silhouette: beveled crest, two upward-swept
side flourishes and a compact plinth. Adapt materials and accents to each
character's palette; do not make every award gold or add a generic trophy cup.
The mechanic symbol stays larger, brighter and more readable than its subdued
backing. Preserve each state's distinct geometry, including paired variants.
The complete symbol, crest, flourishes and pedestal share the existing outer
safety margin. Flat key color remains visible around and through the object.

Infernis Burn and ordinary Tizu/personal shields reuse delivered Burn/Ward art.
Existing9 family/Rose icons stay archived and available for independent effects;
none is deleted or silently repurposed into a new native mechanic.
Aurora's forecast is exact enemy-skill text, not a separate illustrated screen.
Quarry/Survey share one symbol each across targets; numeric progress stays text.

All prompts are reference-free, opaque/no-glow cutouts with continuous margins.
Background keys are exempt from subject palette locks. Current VFX remain
procedural animations; these PNGs are status symbols, not animation sprite sheets.
Nothing is wired or installed until original images are delivered and reviewed.
