LAST LIGHT - ELEMENTAL DUNGEONS, SOAR AND DELVE DROP RATES
Snapshot: October 27, 2026 - D-428 smooth Prismatica across activities

Current implemented drop rates, including ordinary stages, final bosses and
EX+ Calamity chests.

1. ELEMENTAL DUNGEONS
====================

The same rules apply to all six dungeons. Materials match the dungeon's element.
Every unlocked material rarity drops at 100% per defeated enemy. Each starts
at 1-2 when it unlocks and grows linearly to its confirmed Stage 35 range.
Whole-number rounding creates plateaus, never decreasing quantities.

Material | Unlock stage | Stage 35 quantity
Seed     | 1            | 8-9
Bloom    | 1            | 5-6
Shard    | 6            | 4-5
Crest    | 12           | 2-3
Heart    | 19           | 2
Soul     | 25           | 1-2

For current stage s, unlock stage u and final endpoints a,b:
p = (s-u)/(35-u)
minimum = round(1+(a-1)*p)
maximum = round(2+(b-2)*p)
Soul stays 1-2. Every integer in each inclusive range is equally likely.

Prismatica drops at 100% and also grows linearly across stages:
minimum = round(5+295*(s-1)/34)
maximum = round(10+590*(s-1)/34)

NO FINAL-BOSS OR INTERMEDIATE-BOSS REWARD MULTIPLIER applies to dungeons.
Stage 35 simply uses the final endpoints, including 300-600 Prismatica.
The 35-stage enemy level/stat curve and material unlock stages are unchanged.

2. SOAR TO HEAVEN AND DELVE INTO THE ABYSS
=========================================

Both have 15 ordinary stages, with enemies ranging from Lv.50 to Lv.120.
The same normalized level-anchor curve is compressed across 15 stages.
Bosses occur at stages 5, 10 and 15.

SPECIALTY MATERIALS
-------------------

Both used specialties drop independently at 100% per defeated enemy.

Mode  | Material                      | First available     | Ordinary quantity
Soar  | Crown of the Scarlet Oath     | Stage 1 - Lv.50     | 1-2 initially
Soar  | Chalice of the Undying Dawn   | Stage 1 - Lv.50     | 1-2 initially
Delve | Heart of the Shattered Void   | Stage 1 - Lv.50     | 1-2 initially
Delve | Hourglass of Endless Wrath    | Stage 1 - Lv.50     | 1-2 initially

Dawnsteel of Judgment and Voidfang of Ruin no longer drop. Existing holdings
and legacy weapon bonuses are preserved.

Each endpoint grows linearly with stage progress and is rounded:
minimum = round(1 + 10 * (stage - 1) / 14)

The final boss does NOT apply another material multiplier.

Elemental Seeds, Blooms, Shards, Crests, Hearts and Souls: 0% throughout both
modes, including their EX+ chests. Their named specialty materials above are
separate items.

ELEMENTAL-DEVOURERS
-------------------

Mode  | Capture reward                         | Chance per defeated enemy
Soar  | The defeated Dawnthorn Slime form      | 50%
Delve | The defeated Wraththorn Slime form     | 50%

The encountered form progresses as follows:

Stages | Form
1-3    | Common
4-5    | Uncommon
6-8    | Rare
9-10   | Epic
11-13  | Legendary
14-15  | Omnic

Captures join at up to Lv.60. Final bosses retain the 50% capture chance.
The 5x multiplier does not multiply captures.

PRISMATICA
-----------

- 100% per defeated enemy.
- Starts at 6-12 at Lv.50.
- Scales linearly by stage, rounding each endpoint independently.
- Stage 15's final boss gives 500-1,000, without a boss multiplier.
- minimum = round(6+494*(stage-1)/14)
- maximum = round(12+988*(stage-1)/14)

NULL-PRISMATICA
---------------

Below Lv.80, the chance is 0%. From Lv.80 onward, these probabilities
interpolate linearly with enemy level:

Outcome       | Lv.80 reference  | Lv.100 reference  | Lv.120 final boss - Stage 15
Nothing       | 90%              | 82.5%             | 75%
Small payout  | 1 at 8%          | 1 at 9%           | 5 at 10%
Medium payout | 2 at 1.5%        | 2 at 5.75%        | 10 at 10%
Large payout  | 3 at 0.5%        | 3 at 2.75%        | 15 at 5%

The final-boss quantities already include 5x. Its overall chance of receiving
any Null-Prismatica is 25%.
The level references illustrate the unchanged interpolation; the new actual
stage levels are listed above. Premium drops first become possible at
Stage 10 (Lv.81).

3. EX+ CALAMITY BOSSES AND CHESTS
================================

A defeated Calamity boss drops exactly one corresponding chest at 100%.
It replaces ordinary kill loot: no additional ordinary currency, material
or capture rolls occur.

GUARANTEED CONTENTS WHEN OPENING THE CHEST
-----------------------------------------

These base rewards apply to both Elemental Dungeon and Soar/Delve chests:

Guaranteed reward   | EX+ Slayer | EX+ Master | EX+ Omnipotent
Prismatica          | 100,000    | 250,000    | 500,000
Null-Prismatica     | 50         | 70         | 100
Calamitic Tokens    | 1          | 3          | 8
Random loot rolls   | 3          | 5          | 7

Soar/Delve chests additionally guarantee their own mode's highest-form Devourer:

Chest        | Soar: Omnic Dawnthorn | Delve: Omnic Wraththorn
Slayer       | 1 copy                | 1 copy
Master       | 2 copies              | 2 copies
Omnipotent   | 3 copies              | 3 copies

SOAR/DELVE CHEST: EACH RANDOM LOOT ROLL
--------------------------------------

Mode  | Material                      | Chance per roll | Quantity
