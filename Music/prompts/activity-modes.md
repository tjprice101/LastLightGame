# Current activity-mode music

**Status:**16 original SUNO cue briefs; no audio or runtime integration.
Together with the six regional briefs and Main Theme, the library has23 cues.

Copy one **Style prompt** at a time, enable instrumental generation where
available, and leave lyrics empty. Each prompt is standalone. All follow the
[shared sound and review contract](../README.md):170 BPM orchestral rock/metal,
guitar as singer, no human lead voice or speech, optional wordless choir.
Arrangement notes are creative directions, not sung text or gameplay changes.

These are themes for whole activities, not one track per numbered encounter.
For optional later boss edits, develop the same theme with denser orchestra
and guitar harmony; do not silently create an unrelated soundtrack or claim
automatic loop/stage switching exists.

## Training - Grassy Field

**Cue ID:** training-battle

### Style prompt

Instrumental epic orchestral rock/metal, 170 BPM, open grassland determination,
adventurous soul-searching wonder with formidable battle energy. Electric lead
guitar is the singer, masculine phrasing and wide soaring melodies; no lead
vocals, lyrics or speech. Solo nocturne piano, airy open-sky pads and distant
wordless choir build gradually into crushing drums and tight rhythm guitars.
Verses alternate delicate piano over spacious toms with shimmering leads.
Refrains bloom into double-time kicks, stacked wordless choir and sweeping
strings. Near-silent celestial bridge, then two-guitar harmony finale,
orchestra swells and cinematic hits. Dynamic, gentle, awe-inspiring; clear
repeatable melodic identity, less apocalyptic than a final confrontation.

### Arrangement and identity

Open horizons and patient resolve. Keep the refrain memorable and the middle
passages breathable for repeated battles; use a gentle ending rather than
implying a final clear of the endless mode.
