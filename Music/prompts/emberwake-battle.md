# Emberwake Battle Theme

**Cue ID:** emberwake-battle  
**Region:** Emberwake March / Infernic / Wrath  
**Status:** generation brief only; no audio or runtime integration.

## Style prompt

Instrumental epic orchestral rock/metal at 170 BPM, volcanic grandeur, disciplined
wrath, awe-inspiring and intimidating. Electric lead guitar as the singer,
masculine low-register phrases rising into soaring bends; no lead vocals,
lyrics or speech. Solo nocturne piano, airy ash-like synth pads and distant
wordless choir; gradual march into crushing drums, tight palm-muted rhythm
guitars, low brass and urgent strings. Verses alternate delicate piano over
spacious toms with shimmering lead lines. Refrains explode with stacked wordless
choirs, double-time kicks and wide heroic guitar melodies. Near-silent celestial
bridge, then blazing two-guitar harmony finale, orchestral swells and cinematic
hits. Dynamic, majestic, metal, gentle interludes; melody remains clear.

## Arrangement and identity

- **Opening:** sparse piano embers and a distant choir, with low sustained pads.
- **Build:** measured toms, angular guitar pulses and low brass accumulate force;
  intimidation comes from deliberate weight, not nonstop noise.
- **Verses:** alternate tender piano with stern, short guitar answers. Keep
  silence between phrases so the next entrance has impact.
- **Refrains:** blazing, broad lead melody above double-time kick patterns,
  tightly synchronized rhythm guitars and ascending orchestral lines.
- **Bridge:** withdraw almost everything; celestial pads and faint choir hold
  a fragile piano fragment before the pulse returns.
- **Finale:** paired guitars widen the refrain into harmony. Orchestra surges
  and spaced cinematic hits make the scale monumental, then a controlled
  instrumental tail preserves the tension.

**Review target:** wrath with purpose and human vulnerability. No screams,
growls, sung words or relentless maximum-volume compression.
