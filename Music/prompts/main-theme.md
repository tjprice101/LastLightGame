# Main Theme

**Cue ID:** main-theme  
**Status:** generation brief only; no audio or runtime integration.

## Style prompt

Instrumental epic orchestral rock/metal, 170 BPM, awe-inspiring cosmic pilgrimage,
intimidating grandeur with tender humanity. Electric lead guitar is the singer:
resonant masculine register, lyrical bends and wide soaring melodies, NO human
lead voice, lyrics or speech. Solo nocturne piano, airy synth pads and distant
wordless choir introduce a searching motif; gradual build into crushing drums
and tight rhythm guitars. Verses alternate delicate piano, spacious toms and
shimmering lead lines. Huge refrains: stacked wordless choirs, double-time kicks,
strings, brass and singing guitar. Near-silent celestial synth/choir bridge;
climactic dual-guitar harmony finale, orchestra swells and cinematic hits.
Majestic, dynamic, gentle, mysterious; clear melody above dense instrumentation.

## Arrangement and identity

- **Opening:** isolated piano asks a small, unfinished melodic question.
  Airy pads suggest boundless space; choir remains distant and wordless.
- **Build:** strings take fragments of that question before toms and disciplined
  rhythm guitars establish the170 BPM pulse. Save full drums for the reveal.
- **Verse A:** fragile nocturne piano and spacious toms, with a low, expressive
  lead guitar answering rather than overpowering it.
- **Refrain A:** the question expands into a wide, memorable guitar theme,
  supported by double-time kicks, orchestra and stacked choir.
- **Verse B / refrain B:** shimmer and tension grow; retain the same recognizable
  motif instead of replacing it with unrelated solos.
- **Bridge:** near-silence, celestial pads, a faint wordless choir and one piano
  phrase. Leave room for uncertainty in the search.
- **Finale:** two guitars harmonize the main theme while strings/brass swell
  and cinematic hits punctuate its largest statement. Return to a gentle,
  suspended piano ending, suggesting a journey still ahead.

**Review target:** immense wonder rather than a victory anthem. The guitar,
not a vocalist, carries the emotional narrative. No story spoilers.
