## Style prompt

Instrumental epic orchestral rock/metal, 170 BPM, ancient living-forest grandeur,
joy and life with intimidating rooted strength. Electric lead guitar is the
singer, resonant masculine phrasing, warm lyrical bends and wide soaring
melodies; no lead vocals, lyrics or speech. Solo nocturne piano, airy organic
synth pads and distant wordless choir grow into crushing drums and tight
rhythm guitars. Verses alternate delicate branching piano over spacious toms
with shimmering lead lines. Refrains bloom with stacked wordless choirs,
double-time kicks, rich strings, brass and singing guitar. Near-silent
celestial bridge; climactic two-guitar harmony finale, orchestral swells and
cinematic hits. Awe-inspiring, majestic, dynamic, metal, gentle; formidable,
not cute or jaunty.

## Arrangement and identity

- **Opening:** a warm piano seed motif unfolds into branching phrases, with
  soft pads and distant wordless choir.
- **Build:** deep toms, sustained low strings and tight guitar ostinatos grow
  steadily. Rooted weight supports the expanding melody.
- **Verses:** tender nocturne piano alternates with warm guitar answers and
  shimmering lines. Preserve spaciousness and a sense of living movement.
- **Refrains:** a broad, life-affirming lead melody blooms above double-time
  kicks and formidable rhythm guitars; choir and strings magnify its scale.
- **Bridge:** near-silent celestial pads, faint choir and a delicate piano
  reprise suggest vulnerable life beneath the armor.
- **Finale:** interwoven two-guitar harmony, massive orchestral swells and
  cinematic hits create an ancient, indomitable presence. End with the small
  piano motif still alive.

**Review target:** joy as resilience and flourishing life, not lighthearted
comic music. Gentle passages and intimidating peaks must both remain audible.
