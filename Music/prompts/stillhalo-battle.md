  piano; guitar answers are patient, spacious and unmistakably strong.
- **Refrains:** a broad ascending melody holds steady above intense drums.
  Use suspended harmonies and stately brass rather than frantic agitation.
- **Bridge:** nearly silent pads and choir leave one unresolved piano question.
- **Finale:** harmonized guitars defend that theme amid monumental swells;
  let a gentle suspended tail remind the listener the soul search continues.
