# Optional passive and charge accents

All16 passive names below match current kits. These are **optional production
briefs**, not new activations. Set looping off. Quiet0.4-0.7-second accents should
not compete with ability impacts. No constant loops or false effect cues.
Follow the [shared contract](README.md).

## ember-passive - Infernis / Unbroken Ember

**Candidate:** actual personal Ember gain from eligible Burn damage. The
low-health damage bonus itself is not a repeated cast.
```text
A tiny isolated furnace ember catching: dry spark tick, warm compact
ignition puff and short rounded heat resonance. A quiet stored-charge
confirmation, not a full fire attack. No music, voice, breath or ambience.
```
