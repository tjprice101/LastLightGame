# Calyra: moodboard-led character art proposal

**Status:** art-only proposal; identity, names and prompts await owner review.
No gameplay kit, banner, activity, generated image, intake or live character
is included.

## Identity first

**Calyra** is a proposed adult Infernic Element-Bearer and kiln-glazier. She is
precise, warm and stubborn: she turns anger at abandonment into the patient
craft of restoring the stained-glass windows that shelter an ash-district
community. Her narrative role is a shelter-maker and defender, not an assigned
combat class.

Her visual signature is a dark-auburn double-loop braid, amber eyes, an ivory
glass hairpin, a blackened-bronze glassblowing cane and petal-shaped kiln-glass
sculptures. The distinct palette is cinder black, vermilion, copper, pearl
ivory and amber. Her powers are opaque, sharply painted glass ribbons and
faceted petals rather than a generic fire aura. The design keeps the roster's
compact anime/cel renderer, eyes-only face, complete silhouette and solid-key
cutout contract.

## Prompt formula

Each block is a single copy-ready full-body portrait prompt. The six exact
owner-supplied IDs are mapped to their matching evolution with Midjourney's
`--profile` parameter. They are scoped to this concept; do not infer additional
IDs or apply them to existing characters. This draft **supplements** the
shared evolution-matched `--sref`/`--sw 150` suffix to preserve the established
renderer while adding the owner's per-evolution moodboard. If those signed
reference links have expired, request refreshed links rather than substituting.

The moodboard contents are not visible from the IDs alone, so these prompts do
not claim to have inspected them or predict their output. Review generated
pixels before approving or installing any art. `--profile` is listed as a
Midjourney parameter alias in the [official Parameter List](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32859204029709-Parameter-List);
all parameters are placed at the end of each prompt.
