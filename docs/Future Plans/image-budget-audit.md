### Transparent estimation model

The official guidance says an image prompt usually consumes about1 GPU minute,
with variations by model, aspect ratio, quality, references and task. Creative/
Subtle upscales are listed at roughly2 minutes; simple grid separation is not
the same as an expensive detail upscale. These are **planning proxies**, not
measurements of our niji6/reference150 workflow.

- Lean case:2 generation attempts per accepted source, no paid upscale =2 min/source.
- Working case:3 attempts + a2-minute upscale on half the accepted images =4 min/source.
- Heavy revision case:5 attempts + a2-minute upscale on every accepted image =7 min/source.
- A four-image result grid still usually means one prompt job. We assume only
  one usable source per attempt cycle; four candidates do not automatically
  fulfill four different asset requirements.
- Measure10-20 representative portrait/icon/Master/arena jobs with `/info`
  before booking the whole queue. Retry rate and acceptance dominate cost.
- Keep the approved model/style/references; do not switch models, add fake
  single-image modifiers or promise Draft support/cost savings for niji6.
