# Black Diamonds Against The Light

**Status:** Confirmed design and specification for the "Black Diamonds Against The Light" special event and banner expansion.

## Overview & Theme

"Black Diamonds Against The Light" introduces an ancient, austere order of warriors and mystics who harness the indestructible, light-refracting properties of **Black Diamonds**—crystallized carbon birthed under primordial pressure, imbued with contrasting elemental currents and sealed with ritual black and white face and body etchings. 

All characters share:
- **Pale white skin** with striking, geometric black and white ritual etchings across their faces and bodies (like ritual skin art).
- **Gemstone-faceted apparel**: Tailored black and white garments cut into sharp planar facets reminiscent of polished raw diamonds, subtly glowing with their innate elemental frequencies.
- **Progressive armoring & final transcendence (Omnic / Evo. 6)**:
  - Form-fitting and monumental black and white metal armor with intricate geometric articulation.
  - Opaque black and red energy ribbons and rings coiling around them.
  - Floating, orbiting black diamond gemstones charged with elemental power circling the character, with signature weapons fully displayed in dynamic combat poses.

### 1. Dravok (Male · 6★ · Infernic)
- **Title (Evo. 1)**: Ashen Initiate, Dravok
- **Evo Titles**:
  1. Ashen Initiate
  2. Cleaved Obsidian
  3. Pyre Vanguard
  4. Dread-Facet Warlord

  6. The Diamond Beyond the Flame
- **Description**: A battle-hardened warrior whose pale skin and ritual black and white etchings contrast against the roaring inferno within. He swings a colossal two-handed black diamond greatcleaver.

- **Skill 1 - Obsidian Cleave** (Cooldown: 2):

- **Last Flare - Cataclysm of Black Fire** (Cooldown: 0, All Enemies):
  - Unleashes 340% Infernic damage to every living enemy. Bypasses 30% of target defense and inflicts heavy Black Cinder (20 damage/phase). Dravok recovers next turn.

## Event Mission: "Eclipse of Black Diamonds"

The dedicated special event mission features **35 stages** scaling from enemy level **80 to 140**.

### Structure & Progression
- **Stages 1-34**: 2 enemies per stage.
- **Boss Stages (Every 5th stage: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35)**: 1 formidable boss enemy.
- **Enemy Progression**: 6 escalating forms of the crystalline colossus **Kharonite**:

| Form | Stages | Enemy Name | Rarity | Sale Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-5 | Kharonite | Common | 1 Shard of Black Diamond |
| 2 | 6-10 | Facet of the Dark Sun, Kharonite | Uncommon | 2 Facet of Black Diamond |
| 3 | 11-15 | Knight of the Cleaved Diamond, Kharonite | Rare | 3 Cluster of Black Diamond |
| 4 | 16-21 | Colossus of the Obsidian Core, Kharonite | Epic | 4 Brilliant of Black Diamond |
| 5 | 22-28 | Monarch of the Black Prism, Kharonite | Legendary | 5 Heart of Black Diamond |
| 6 | 29-35 | The Sovereign Beyond the Diamond Light, Kharonite | Omnic | 6 Matrix of Black Diamond |

### Drops & Captures
- **Captures**: 20% independent capture chance per defeat, **only at enemy levels ≤ 120** (stages 1-23). Above level 120 (stages 24-35), enemies are too condensed to capture.
- **Materials**: 6 event-exclusive materials drop across stages:
  1. **Shard of Black Diamond** (Common) - Unlocks at Lv. 80
  2. **Facet of Black Diamond** (Uncommon) - Unlocks at Lv. 90
  3. **Cluster of Black Diamond** (Rare) - Unlocks at Lv. 100
  4. **Brilliant of Black Diamond** (Epic) - Unlocks at Lv. 110
  5. **Heart of Black Diamond** (Legendary) - Unlocks at Lv. 120
  6. **Matrix of Black Diamond** (Omnic) - Unlocks at Lv. 130
- **Currency**: Scaled Prismatica drops per kill; no Null-Prismatica or clear bonuses.

## Special Banner: "Black Diamonds Against The Light"

- **Draw Cost**: 10 Null-Prismatica per draw.
- **Availability**: Continuous special banner without artificial expiration.
- **Banner Odds**:
  - **6★ Element-Bearers (Dravok, Talise, Malakor)**: **1.1% total** (0.3667% each).
  - **5★ Element-Bearers (Vespera, Theron, Selene)**: **1.5% total** (0.5% each).
  - **Non-Character Pool (97.4%)**: Bundles of Shard, Facet, and Cluster of Black Diamond (50:30:17 normalized weights).
- **Banner Pity**:
  - Uses the same independent **80 / 150** per-banner pity system as the other banners (D-208):
    - **80 Highest-Star Guarantee**: Guarantees a 6★ Element-Bearer (Dravok, Talise, or Malakor) on the 80th draw without a 6★ pull. Any 6★ result (natural or duplicate) resets this counter.
    - **150 Unowned Highest-Star Guarantee**: Guarantees an **unowned 6★ Element-Bearer** from the banner on the 150th draw without a new 6★ character. Pulling a new 6★ character resets both the 150 and 80 counters.
    - **All-Owned Fallback**: If all three 6★ characters are already owned when 150 pity is reached, it awards the standard 6★ duplicate conversion (Omnic Kharonite at Lv. 80) and resets both counters.
    - **Priority & Odds**: When both 80 and 150 guarantees are due at once, 150 takes priority. Eligible guaranteed characters split equally (1/3 each initially).
    - **5★ Non-Reset**: Pulling a 5★ character (Vespera, Theron, or Selene) does not reset the 80 or 150 highest-star pity counters.
    - **Atomic Persistence**: Draw cost (10 Null-Prismatica), result/duplicate conversion, and new pity counters commit together in one atomic write; failed or rejected draws never advance pity.
- **Duplicate Conversion**:
  - Duplicate 6★ Element-Bearer awards an **Omnic Kharonite at Lv. 80** with its final-form kit (usable in squads or saleable for 6 Matrix of Black Diamond).
  - Duplicate 5★ Element-Bearer awards a **Legendary Kharonite at Lv. 65** (saleable for 5 Heart of Black Diamond).
