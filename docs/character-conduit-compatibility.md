# Character / Conduit compatibility (D-382)

Generated by `tools/build_phase_zero_conduit_catalog.mjs` from the authoritative
[138-entry catalog](conduit-catalog.txt). This replaces the historical133-item
matrix; do not regenerate this document with the retired redesign generator.

- All90 ordinary-slot entries retain their saved IDs, ownership, upgrades,
  acquisition and equipment restrictions. Common/Rare/Legendary/Calamitic
  entries are universal; ordinary Omnics still require the matching combat
  element and share the four-Omnic limit. The48 named Masters remain fixed,
  bearer-only and non-upgradeable in their separate slot.
- Effects require their actual named action, origin, target and timing.
  Equipment never invents a missing captured skill, native resource or debuff.
  Generated effects do not recursively trigger manual Attack Action effects.
- Native primary impacts aggregate actual damage once per activation. Delayed
  Seeds retain their planting snapshots; Burn retains source ownership and its
  application-time level multiplier. No converted packet scales twice.
- Native Shields retain owners and expiry. Pale Bulwark changes damage covered,
  not actual Shield consumed; mixed owners cannot earn a sole-owner full block.
- Beneficial ordinary percentages grow10% of base per upgrade. Penalties,
  conditions, proc odds, durations and action counts remain fixed. Skill
  Efficiency retains its50% cap and1/70 Gauge floors; Skill Duration has no
  global equipment cap, but individual status safety limits remain.
- Rules6 restarts old encounter snapshots through the existing compatibility
  path, preserving frozen investments and saved rewards. Settings/resume keeps
  current cast outcomes, Shield layers and Stasis clocks.

`phase-zero-interactions.test.ts` and `conduit-rework.test.ts`. Historical
