# Character / Conduit compatibility

Originally generated by `tools/build_conduit_redesign.mjs` from the authoritative
[133-item effect roster](conduit-redesign.md). D-359 corrections below are
maintained alongside that roster and the live runtime; reconcile current
approved effects before rerunning the historical generator.
The five later [Calamitic creations](calamitic-conduits.md) use their own typed
registry and exhaustive six-level tests in `src/game/calamitic-conduits.test.ts`;
the full equipment catalog is now 90 ordinary plus 48 Masters.
The [native identity contract](character-identity-redesign.md) replaces retired
personal counters; no item manufactures missing native actions, charges or Burn.

- **Retained**: its existing trigger/outcome remains legal on a kit that owns
  that action/status. This does not promise every item is useful on every bearer.
- **Adapted**: a targeting, timing or retired-native dependency is explicitly
  replaced by the complete current effect below. All stable IDs, ownership,
  counts, levels, art, rarity, acquisition and prices are retained.
- Ordinary effects are available to eligible captured Elemental-Devourers.
  Named Masters remain bearer-only. No automatic unequips or account writes.
- Native primary impacts aggregate hit totals once per manual activation.
  Fixed native follow-ups and generated effects never recursively earn native
  action resources or activate another equipment action.
- Delayed Seeds snapshot Instruction/Graft at planting. Springwell applies once
  at payout; Covercharge/Triage settle the completed payout once, without
  replaying a skill or Master action. A fallen planter pays out nothing.
- Storm Clock remains its own three-critical equipment clock, not Vaelor's Beat
  or retired Tempest. It works for eligible Atmospheric bearers/captures.
- Timed native shields retain owners and phase expiry; generated plates remain
  Conduit-origin. A full absorption by mixed shield owners does not earn Tizu's
  own-shield full-block passive.

Validation: `src/game/conduit-roster.test.ts` exercises every ordinary item at
all six upgrade levels with/without the named equipment and every Master
with/without its bearer-specific item. Connected native-action and shared
incoming/healing cases are in `conduit-native-compatibility.test.ts`,
`conduit-redesign.test.ts` and `native-kits.test.ts`. D-359 adds real-action
and all-six-upgrade stat regressions in `conduit-integration.test.ts`, including
slot ordering, Burn persistence, full Tailwind spending, Normal Attack hooks,
first-skill-only multi-impact parry and Bruno's Defense-scaled Shield floor.
