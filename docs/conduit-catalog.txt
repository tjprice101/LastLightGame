SQUAD DESIGN REVISION
D-382 replaces all 10 Common, 15 Rare, 15 Legendary and five Calamitic effects.
D-383 refreshes all 45 ordinary Omnic and 48 Master abilities. Owner55 designs are the baseline; the remaining38 retain distinct native/elemental mechanics with tailored stat packages.
Masters remain character-specific and build on their actual native abilities.
Calamitic effects emphasize one clear squad-changing mechanic, not long chains
of conditions. Practical usefulness is a design goal, not a tested balance claim.

138 Conduits: 90 ordinary-slot Conduits and 48 named Masters.
Sorted by rarity (lowest to highest), then alphabetically by name.
Masters are Omnic, not a separate rarity, and appear in the Omnic section.

ELEMENT KEY
Actual gameplay element: an existing elemental equipment restriction.
Named bearer's actual element: the Master belongs only to that Element-Bearer.
Suggested thematic element: an editorial recommendation based on name, ability
and theme. The Conduit is currently universal; this is NOT a gameplay change
or an approved elemental restriction. Botanic includes earth/stone themes;
Atmospheric includes lightning; Tranquilitic includes light/peace themes.

Player-facing descriptions show current values, not upgrade formulas or repeated

PHASE 0 - STAT CONTRACT
Existing Health, Defense, Direct Attack, Critical Rate, Critical Damage
Multiplier and Gauge Capacity growth remains unchanged. New base values:
100% Skill Power, +0 turns Skill Duration, 0% Skill Efficiency and 100%
Elemental Gauge Gain, with no level-based growth for these four stats.

1. Health: maximum HP. Healing cannot exceed it unless explicitly stored.
2. Defense: reduces incoming Direct Damage BEFORE it reaches Shield or Health.
   Use the existing diminishing-return Defense formula, not flat invulnerability.
   Burn and explicitly stated damage that bypasses Defense remain exceptions.
3. Direct Attack: baseline attack value for Normal Attack, damaging skills,
   critical hits and effects that explicitly scale from Direct Attack.
4. Critical Damage Multiplier: total multiplier on an eligible critical hit.
   A +10% multiplier bonus increases its base by 10%, not by +0.10 flat.
5. Critical Rate: chance to critically hit. +X% Critical Rate adds directly
   to the chance; the existing Elemental Crit overflow remains.
6. Skill Power: additive effectiveness scaling for Skill 1, Skill 2 and Last
   Flare. Baseline effectiveness is 100%; +15% and +20% produce 135%, not 138%.
   Applies once to authored damage, healing, Shields and scalable status
   potency, including Burn. Replaces separate Elemental Damage scaling.
   Does not change Normal Attack, duration, Gauge rewards, action counts,
   target counts, critical chance, hard-control rules or Conduit proc values.
7. Skill Duration: WHOLE additional turns on timed effects you apply through
   skills. +1 turns a two-turn effect into three turns. It does not make an
   instant attack/heal repeat. It never changes cooldowns, recovery, delayed
   payout timing, stack charges, revives or action grants. Same-owner refreshes
   replace the remaining duration; they do not append turns indefinitely.
8. Skill Efficiency: additive percentage reduction to the skill's Gauge cost.
   +10% and +15% reduce the original cost by 25%; a negative value raises cost.
   Combined reduction cap: 50%; round the final cost up once.
   Skill 1/2 cost at least 1 Gauge and Last Flare at least 70, unless a
   specifically named, one-use effect explicitly overrides that floor.
9. Elemental Gauge Capacity: the amount the bearer can hold, not free Gauge.
   Increasing capacity does not fill the extra space.
10. Elemental Gauge Gain: additive scaling from ALL positive incoming sources,
    including attacks, skills, hits and Conduits. Baseline is 100%; +5% and
    +15% yield 120%. Apply once on receipt and round once, not per modifier.
    Does not discount costs, multiply restored snapshots or refund spent Gauge.
    Conduit grants written as X% Elemental Gauge restore X% of the recipient's
    own maximum capacity before Gauge Gain scaling. Costs and held-Gauge
    thresholds are not percentage grants.

GLOBAL DESIGN CLARIFICATIONS AND SAFETY LIMITS
- Other percentage stat modifiers add within that stat before applying to its
  base. Conditional Skill Power joins the same additive pool, not another
  multiplication. A packet already scaled by Skill Power is not scaled again
  when copied, stored, converted, reflected or released.
- Attack Actions are damaging Normal Attacks, Skill 1, Skill 2 and Last
  Flare activations, not periodic or generated damage. A prohibition on
  critical hits overrides a guaranteed-critical or critical-chance bonus.
- Timed effects use the appropriate player's turn or Enemy Turn clock stated
  in their ability. Equipment Skill Duration bonuses add without a global cap.
  Instant effects and one-use tokens do not acquire duration from this stat.
- Status potency retains its own limits: Weaken cannot exceed 50%. Skill Power
  cannot turn Weaken, delay or replacement skills into permanent enemy lockdown.
- "This turn" ends at the next turn of that side; "next action" tokens expire
  after two player turns unless a shorter duration is stated. Identical
  temporary effects refresh instead of stacking unless a stack cap is stated.
- Battle limits reset with a new encounter, not on Settings, an extra action
  or a revived recipient. Repeated squad-wide auras with the same name use
  only the strongest instance. Gauge ties use formation order; a Gauge
  percentage is current Gauge divided by that character's own capacity.
- Effective healing, actual Shield absorption and actual damage are the
  measurements for triggered effects. Overkill counts only when named.
  Lowest/weakest ally means lowest current HP percentage, then formation order.
- Conduit-generated strikes/heals/Shields/reflections cannot trigger another
  Conduit proc or earn native resources. Gauge Gain still applies once to a
  positive Gauge receipt, but overflow transfers cannot transfer again.
- A Master modifying a native skill, Seed, trap, Cyclone or Foundation keeps
  that ability's native identity. A separate generated follow-up is still
  excluded from proc chains. Repeated impacts count as one Attack Action.
- Normal Attacks and skills must be manually chosen to trigger equipment.
  Named Masters may explicitly trigger from their bearer's native delayed
  effects. Squad auras apply only while their bearer is alive.
- Bonuses to an ally's next action or skill hold one copy per named Conduit
  per recipient. Multi-target casts grant each recipient a bonus at most once.
  Unless specified, receiving a new bonus refreshes it, not its magnitude.
- Defense reductions add, capped at 50%; a skill's own Defense ignore then
  applies to the remaining Defense and is capped at 75%. Neither changes
  bypass damage or special encounter immunity.
- When a skill's cost depends on its target, evaluate it against the selected
  target before paying. Conditional skill bonuses use the activation snapshot.
  Follow-up heals and Shields arrive after the triggering attack resolves.
- Only HP actually lost to enemy attacks fills damage reserves; self-costs,
  redirected damage, delayed debt and generated damage cannot fill them.
  Delaying damage changes its timing, never its total. Each damage packet
  can be delayed only once, regardless of squad equipment.
- One current Shield per recipient retains the existing replacement rules.
  When two Shields would be granted simultaneously, keep the larger rather
  than letting a smaller Conduit follow-up replace a stronger native Shield.
  Unused healing pools and expiring buffs do not become permanent reserves.
- A passive stat bonus applies continuously, not once per hit.
- Each manual activation can earn a particular extra action at most once.
  No chain of extra actions can grant further extra actions. A character can
  spend at most one granted extra action per player turn, across all sources.
- Rescue and revival effects are once per recipient per Battle across the
  squad, regardless of how many copies or different rescue items are present.
  A rescue leaves the recipient vulnerable to subsequent damage; no immunity.
- Non-Defense damage reductions combine multiplicatively and cannot reduce
  Direct Damage below 20% of its pre-reduction amount. Absorbed Shields can
  still prevent HP loss, but finite Shields are consumed normally.
- Offensive effects never fabricate a missing native Burn, Weaken, resource,
  healing action or Shield. Named Masters modify existing authored abilities.
- This contract is the implementation target, not a deployment record.
  Existing account holdings and rarity do not change.
