# Moon and Sunshade: identity-first character design

## Status and approval boundary

**Phase2, step1: identity proposals (2026-11-04). Not approved or playable.**
The owner authorizes continuing the roadmap, not skipping individual character
review. This document proposes twelve identities before full appearances,
numeric kits, Conduits or prompts. No runtime registrations, assets, drops,
banner odds or launch dates are changed.

Each banner retains four5-stars (two male/two female) and two6-stars
(one male/one female). Both group themes are confirmed requirements; names,
individual lore, role assignments and the interpretations below are proposals.
No new element, creator, soul discovery, resolved war or Story chapter is
introduced. Moonlight and sunlight are aesthetic motifs, not combat elements.

## Banner #2: Glow of the Moon's Light

**Confirmed theme:** black-and-white shimmering moonlight angelic warriors,
shiny metallic armor, the moon's strength and space's beauty and regality.

**Proposed shared identity:** the Witnesses of the Unfinished Night, a mortal
order that records promises made to people still missing. Their angelic
appearance expresses deliberate craft and elemental manifestations, not a
new divine species or proof that Let has returned. Moonlight reveals what
their weapons are protecting; it does not make all six peaceful or identical.
The order name and mortal-order interpretation require owner review.

### Maerion — male,5-star, Oceanic Healer

- **Who:** a battlefield surgeon who preserves the names on ruined ferry
  tokens. Reserved, meticulous and unexpectedly gentle with frightened people.
- **Lore:** after an evacuation fleet failed to return, he refused to mark
  its passengers dead merely to close a ledger. He follows reports of Let's
  soul while treating those left behind, without claiming the missing survived.
- **Distinct identity:** care as patient, precise work, not a generic water
  priest. His grief is responsibility, not helplessness.
- **Signature to develop:** a crescent surgical polearm and measured ribbons
  of silver-black water, arranged like the protective ribs of a lunar vessel.
- **Gameplay direction, not a kit:** deliberate restoration/recovery planning;
  not Tizu's shield tank or Nerithe's direct attacker.
- **Omnic name-fit test:** a majestic keeper of the unreturned must visibly
  shelter a remembered voyage, not merely hold a blue staff.

### Caevren — male,5-star, Atmospheric Tank

- **Who:** an observatory sentinel who catches falling instruments before
  anyone notices the tower shaking. Dry humor; stillness under pressure.
- **Lore:** he held a ruined sky-bridge open during an evacuation and stayed
  behind to recover the records needed to search for the missing.
- **Distinct identity:** an immovable astronomical counterweight, not another
  storm swordsman, musician or feathered wind caster.
- **Signature to develop:** one massive counterweight flail, orbital bracing
  rings and compressed wind vanes with a disciplined, protective silhouette.
- **Gameplay direction:** intercept and redistribute pressure through active
  positioning choices; exact targeting rules need review.
- **Omnic test:** an immense celestial anchor keeping the night from falling.

### Vespera — female,5-star, Infernic Support

- **Who:** a night-watch signal officer who refuses to extinguish the final
  beacon while anyone remains beyond the walls. Stern, practical, fiercely loyal.
- **Lore:** she once kept an evacuation signal burning against orders to
  abandon a district. Her Wrath is directed at preventable abandonment.
- **Distinct identity:** fire as coordination and defiance, not Infernis's
  greatsword aggression or Caldrex's furnace enclosure.
- **Signature to develop:** a bladed signal standard, silver-white flame
  pennants and blackened mirrored armor; no accidental orange Moon palette.
- **Gameplay direction:** prepare allies' offensive opportunities rather than
  become a third Infernic damage specialist.
- **Omnic test:** a winged procession of unextinguished signal flames, all
  visibly originating from her standard.

### Ilyss — female,5-star, Chaotic Healer

- **Who:** a funerary restorer who repairs memorials but will not promise
  resurrection. Warm, candid and impatient with false consolation.
- **Lore:** she learned to separate destructive remnants from people still
  living after a sanctuary collapsed. She seeks answers, not dominion over death.
- **Distinct identity:** healing by carefully ending what is harming someone;
  Chaotic remains Death/Destruction, not a renamed Life element.
- **Signature to develop:** paired severance shears, black-silver ribbons and
  broken crescent links arranged into an angelic protective form.
- **Gameplay direction:** proactive sustain through bounded removal/triage
  choices; no resurrection or unrestricted cleanse is assumed.
- **Omnic test:** a regal unbinder of suffering, not a generic dark cleric.

### Asteriel — male,6-star, Botanic Support

- **Who:** the keeper of a night-blooming seed library, who lends its rarest
  seeds instead of hoarding them. Courtly, curious and quietly mischievous.
- **Lore:** when a royal conservatory sealed its gates during famine, he
  distributed its living collection and accepted exile.
- **Distinct identity:** Joy/Life expressed as shared possibility and
  cultivation, not Flora's healing, Myrthen's harvest or Orvella's foundations.
- **Signature to develop:** an unfolding seed-orrery focus, metallic moonflower
  armor and sharply patterned growth paths linking his orbiting seed chambers.
- **Gameplay direction:** cultivate different ally payoffs through deliberate
  choices; avoid another generic Attack-buff rotation.
- **Omnic test:** a magnificent living star-garden whose every orbit carries
  a future he is willing to give away.

### Serapha — female,6-star, Tranquilitic Healer

- **Who:** a former dueling adjudicator who now escorts people through
  ceasefires she knows may fail. Composed, exacting and unwilling to confuse
  silence with peace.
- **Lore:** she laid down a victor's ceremonial blade after a lawful duel
  achieved nothing but another grieving household.
- **Distinct identity:** restoration through protected intervals and mutual
  restraint, not Solenne's bells/Stillness or Aurora's judgment lenses.
- **Signature to develop:** a split-bladed mercy scepter, articulated silver
  mantle and nested lunar arcs that form deliberate safe openings.
- **Gameplay direction:** restorative windows with meaningful timing decisions;
  no automatic invulnerability, revival or stall loop.
- **Omnic test:** a commanding sanctuary in motion, with unmistakable mercy
  in the design rather than a passive angel holding a staff.

## Banner #3: The Angel-blazened Sunshade

**Confirmed theme:** head-to-toe black-and-golden armored warriors, armor
igniting into black-and-orange flames, sunlit glory, ornate and regal designs.

**Proposed shared identity:** the Keepers of the Borrowed Dawn, a mortal order
that protects settlements beneath a shattered solar canopy. They do not own
the sun or worship a newly invented creator. Their heavy black-gold armor
channels their own elements; orange-black ignition is the shared aesthetic,
not permission to change every character's combat element to Infernic.
The order, canopy history and non-Infernic flame interpretation need review.

### Rhaziel — male,5-star, Infernic Healer

- **Who:** a field armorer who returns repaired armor with its dents recorded,
  never polished out of history. Blunt, generous and stubbornly protective.
- **Lore:** after discovering that rushed royal equipment had failed its
  wearers, he opened his forge to people the court would not provision.
- **Distinct identity:** restorative forgecraft driven by anger at neglect,
  not Caldrex's heat-storage tank identity.
- **Signature to develop:** a long forge needle and anvil-gauntlet, black-gold
  articulated armor and controlled orange-black seams joining protective forms.
- **Gameplay direction:** sustain that rewards preparing for incoming pressure;
  distinguish its decisions from Maerion's restoration.
- **Omnic test:** a resplendent armored mender, rebuilding a defense under fire.

### Oryn — male,5-star, Tranquilitic Support

- **Who:** a former ceremonial execution guard who became a mediator before
  carrying out an unjust sentence. Soft-spoken, observant and difficult to hurry.
- **Lore:** he carries an unstruck seal instead of a list of condemned names,
  seeking settlements where opposing parties can actually leave alive.
- **Distinct identity:** Peace as negotiated terms and coordinated restraint,
  not another moon sanctuary, bell keeper or mental domination kit.
- **Signature to develop:** a broad seal-headed polehammer, sunshade shoulder
  plates and interlocking oath arcs traced through orange-black ignition.
- **Gameplay direction:** select a clear ally/enemy condition to alter the next
  exchange; no absolute action denial or forced surrender.
- **Omnic test:** an overwhelming arbiter whose authority creates a way out.

### Thessara — female,5-star, Oceanic Support

- **Who:** a desert reservoir captain who measures every promise in water
  delivered. Sardonic, practical and suspicious of beautiful speeches.
- **Lore:** she diverted her city's ceremonial water procession to refugees,
  then followed the emptied channels to find the villages the court omitted.
- **Distinct identity:** Sadness transformed into equitable provision, not
  another sea monarch, tidal shield user or lunar surgeon.
- **Signature to develop:** a paired-vessel glaive, black-gold reservoir armor,
  sharp water bands passing through flame-lit mechanical-looking armor channels.
- **Gameplay direction:** distribute a bounded team resource with competing
  priorities; no unlimited Gauge engine or copied Nerithe Normal bonus.
- **Omnic test:** the radiant bearer of a moving reservoir, with water readable
  even amid the banner's orange-black armor ignition.

### Elaris — female,5-star, Botanic Attacker

- **Who:** an orchard outrider who cuts firebreaks rather than waiting for
  permission to save the next grove. Adventurous and irreverently optimistic.
- **Lore:** she replanted a burned royal hunting preserve as a public orchard,
  then joined the order to defend roads linking isolated growing communities.
- **Distinct identity:** branching precision and cultivated escape routes,
  not Myrthen's seasons, chain/sickles or Harvest cycle.
- **Signature to develop:** a single branching javelin, layered black-gold
  seedpod armor and rigid growth-lances arranged into a blazing crown.
- **Gameplay direction:** build a precise breach through selected targets;
  she deepens Botanic attacking rather than filling a nonexistent empty role.
- **Omnic test:** a sovereign spear-grove bursting through a burning enclosure.

### Veyr — male,6-star, Chaotic Attacker

- **Who:** a dismantler of siege engines who counts rescued workers rather
  than destroyed machines. Patient, unshowy and unyielding once committed.
- **Lore:** he brought down an occupied war foundry without collapsing the
  refuge hidden beneath it, then refused the title of conqueror.
- **Distinct identity:** destructive precision and controlled structural failure,
  not Crinso's duality blade or Kaelith's fate-cutting Calamitic identity.
- **Signature to develop:** an articulated demolition lance, segmented
  black-gold armor and concentric fracture seams outlined by black-orange flame.
- **Gameplay direction:** dismantle a chosen defense through earned setup;
  no new universal percentage-health execution.
- **Omnic test:** a terrifyingly regal siege-breaker who controls exactly
  what falls and what remains standing.

### Aureth — female,6-star, Atmospheric Tank

- **Who:** the captain who carries the broken canopy's keystone on her back,
  refusing to leave its unprotected settlements to the next ash storm.
  Expansive confidence, decisive humor and no patience for fatalism.
- **Lore:** she organized rival watchtowers into a rescue corridor when the
  solar canopy broke; the order's leadership was a responsibility, not a crown.
- **Distinct identity:** mobile protection through commanding wind channels,
  not Caevren's anchored counterweight or a reskin of Ozriel's wall.
- **Signature to develop:** a sunshade shield and short command blade,
  black-gold canopy armor and immense sharply segmented convection wings.
- **Gameplay direction:** protect a moving squad through chosen exposure/
  redirection windows, distinct from stationary absorption.
- **Omnic test:** a magnificent walking eclipse shelter, holding the burning
  sky open without becoming a building or losing her human silhouette.
