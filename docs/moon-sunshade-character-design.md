## Shared appearance contract

- Same original Infernis/Tizu compact anime/cel renderer for every character:
  rounded head, tiny torso, short limbs, 2.5-3 heads tall, sharp painted facets
  and crisp contours. Eyes are the only anatomical facial features; personality
  comes from eye shape, posture, costume and equipment, not realistic faces.
  No new facial paint, scars, mouths, noses or eyebrows are proposed.
- Each line fixes hair, eye color, skin tone, signature equipment and costume
  motifs across all six forms. There are **no proposed hair/eye/skin changes**.
  The stage tables explicitly list purposeful costume and power changes.
  Added crowns do not replace the underlying hairstyle or hide the eyes.
- Common is genuinely simple, even for6-stars. Uncommon refines craft, Rare
  establishes the full motif, Epic unfolds it, Legendary becomes monumental,
  and Omnic expands that same architecture into a distinct lavish crescendo.
  Six-star acquisition is not permission for an Omnic-looking base.
- Moon uses black-primary and white-accent material with silver mechanics,
  deep-blue and vivid-purple power, and clearly colored moon phases. Metallic
  sheen is painted reflection, not arbitrary rainbow recoloring.
  Sunshade's owner-approved redesign (2026-11-05) uses **black and white as
  primary and secondary costume/equipment colors, never gold**, with **deep
  orange and pearlescent pink elemental energy**. Pearlescence means crisp
  pink/white painted facets, not rainbow sheen, translucency or emitted light.
  Water, growth, fracture, repair and convection retain distinct shapes within
  that energy palette, not their former blue/olive/gold accents. Natural skin,
  hair and eyes are exempt. Common carries one small energy trace; late forms
  build magnificent solar/celestial architecture from the same owner motif.
- Both banners explicitly request armored warriors. Individuality here comes
  from coat, skirt, tabard, collar, plate shape and wing construction, not twelve
  identical cuirasses. Cloth is fully covered and supports movement; Sunshade
  armor covers torso, arms, hands, legs and feet from Common onward.
- Wings are paired elemental/constructed silhouettes, not new species or extra
  limbs. Counts below are **total individual wings**, not pairs. A ring means
  one complete designed loop; arcs are explicitly open. No floating extra people.
- Wings, rings and ribbons remain visibly connected to character/equipment
  motifs rather than generic background explosions. Dense late rear detailing
  retains clear eyes, hands, weapon and narrow open key channels. Pull the entire
  ensemble back uniformly to keep at least15% uninterrupted solid-key clearance
  on every edge; never crop tips or remove design details to fit the canvas.
- Cutout powers are opaque, sharply bounded, non-emissive painted shapes.
  Future portrait negatives include `glow on edges`; no soft bloom, haze or
  transparent veils. Runtime glare/bloom/VFX can be radiant separately.
  These are design specifications, not generated-image guarantees or prompt
  blocks. Evolution titles will be authored later, separately from banner names.

## Banner #2: Glow of the Moon's Light

**Confirmed theme:** black-and-white shimmering moonlight angelic warriors,
shiny metallic armor, the moon's strength and space's beauty and regality.

**Approved shared identity (2026-11-04):** the Witnesses of the Unfinished Night, a mortal
order that records promises made to people still missing. Their angelic
appearance expresses deliberate craft and elemental manifestations, not a
new divine species or proof that Let has returned. Moonlight reveals what
their weapons are protecting; it does not make all six peaceful or identical.
Owner selected this named mortal celestial order and subsequently accepted
the six individual identities and complete appearance/power details.

### Maerion — male,6-star, Oceanic Healer

- **Who:** a battlefield surgeon who preserves the names on ruined ferry
  tokens. Reserved, meticulous and unexpectedly gentle with frightened people.
- **Lore:** after an evacuation fleet failed to return, he refused to mark
  its passengers dead merely to close a ledger. He follows reports of Let's
  soul while treating those left behind, without claiming the missing survived.
- **Distinct identity:** care as patient, precise work, not a generic water
  priest. His grief is responsibility, not helplessness.
- **Signature to develop:** a crescent surgical polearm and measured ribbons
  of silver-black water, arranged like the protective ribs of a lunar vessel.
- **Gameplay direction, not a kit:** deliberate restoration/recovery planning;
  not Tizu's shield tank or Nerithe's direct attacker.
- **Omnic name-fit test:** a majestic keeper of the unreturned must visibly
  shelter a remembered voyage, not merely hold a blue staff.

**Costume identity:** a high-collared black surgeon-admiral coat with white
inner prow panels, silver hull-rib seams and violet crescent clasps, split
below the knee into two boat-prow panels. Plain unlettered ferry
tokens hang from the same left-belt clasp in every form; no readable names.
The single crescent polearm has a long dark shaft and blunt inward silver
curve, not an additional sword or realistic surgical instrument.
