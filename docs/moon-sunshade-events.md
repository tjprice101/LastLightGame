without waiting for Midjourney artwork. All twelve new Element-Bearers are
**six-star**. This supersedes the earlier four5-star/two6-star distribution;
gender balance, identities, six forms, approved appearances and kits remain.
Flagged kit coefficients remain unchanged. No new art is delivered.
Both events stay **inactive with no scheduled window until explicit launch
approval**. Never interpret an empty schedule as an event that already ended.

Reuse **current** Rose mechanics, not its historical rules:

  across reruns. A duplicate bearer converts to15 matching event Bloom,

## Moon: Glow of the Moon's Light

### Signature activity: The Harbor of Unreturned Names

The Witnesses of the Unfinished Night defend a harbor observatory whose
unlettered ferry-token lanterns preserve unresolved promises. Its custodial
Devourers are not departed human souls and do not prove anyone dead or alive.
They have mistaken protection of those records for rejection of every visitor.
The squad opens a passage without resolving the creators' war or a Story chapter.

**Enemy line: Vesperwing**, a Tranquilitic lunar moth Devourer. Keep a single
compact moth body, dark central carapace, white antennae and white-silver wings.
No humanoid face, held weapons or Rose armor. The same crescent ferry-token
chest plate and split tail ribbon identify all six forms. Calm-but-formidable
silhouette progresses toward a vast open harbor gate; not a copied butterfly
character or feathery angel.

| Form | Proposed name | Appearance and powers |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Vesperwing | Two small white moth wings, plain black carapace and one silver crescent plate; a single quiet white wake stroke. |
| Uncommon | Tokenwatch Vesperwing | Same two wings gain silver leading-edge ribs; paired white tail ribbons and two short wake arcs. |
| Rare | Quaykeeper Vesperwing | Four moth wings, black-white segmented thorax plates, crescent rimmed antenna bases and two open silver wake arcs. |
| Epic | Nightlock Vesperwing | Six pleated wing panels, layered polished silver carapace and three interleaved open harbor arcs; body remains compact. |
| Legendary | Unreturned Harbor Vesperwing | Eight monumental ribbed moth wings, dense black-silver token tracery and four open lunar arches, never a building carried on its back. |
| Omnic | Passage Beyond Midnight Vesperwing | Twelve vast paired moth wings retain the Legendary rib architecture; five open crescent passages and dense wake filaments around one clear dark body. |

**Enemy skill direction:** Crescent Quay Strike, scheduled direct single-target
attack using current event skill coefficients. At the existing second-skill
gate, Harbor of Quiet Wings supplies the ordinary second/direct boss-heavy
attack in the existing schedule. No new enemy invulnerability, resurrection,
player Gauge drain or additional drop RNG.

One family: Seed/Bud/Bloom/Crest/Heart/Soul of **Moonwake**. It is exclusive
to Maerion, Caevren, Vespera, Ilyss, Asteriel and Serapha, regardless of their
combat element. The family is crafting matter, not another elemental affinity.

| Bud | Same central capsule; two white folded petal shutters open, silver crescent ribs and a small wake ribbon. |

Vesperwing captures are the same-event infusion fodder. **No extra specialty
item chain** is required: Moonwake materials and selected Vesperwing copies
fulfill the current Rose-format evolution/infusion replacement.

## Sunshade: The Angel-blazened Sunshade

### Signature activity: Beneath the Borrowed Dawn

The Keepers of the Borrowed Dawn reopen rescue routes under the shattered
solar canopy. Devourer wardens endlessly repair obsolete routes and attack
anyone moving their keystones. The squad changes what is protected, not the
world's creators or established elements. The deep-orange/pearlescent-pink ignition
is a visual motif; non-Infernic bearers do not automatically gain Burn.

**Enemy line: Solcarapace**, an Infernic solar scarab Devourer. One black
beetle body with white carapace ridges, six small armored legs and split
wing cases. No humanoid bodies, swords, machine-only provenance or moth
anatomy. A single white wedge-shaped head crest and paired round shoulder
vents persist across all forms. Opaque deep-orange and pearlescent-pink flame folds unfold
behind the wing cases; complete antennae/legs remain contained.

| Form | Proposed name | Appearance and powers |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Solcarapace | Plain black beetle shell, narrow white rims, six short legs and one small deep-orange seam flame with a pink tip. No mature wing array. |
| Uncommon | Dawnvent Solcarapace | White ribwork on the same shell, two opened wing cases, paired orange-pink curls and reinforced white head wedge. |
| Rare | Routeforge Solcarapace | Two broad black-white flight wings, segmented shell shutters, paired vents and two angular deep-orange paths edged pearlescent pink. |
| Epic | Canopyward Solcarapace | Four layered black-white wings and three folded orange-pink flame arcs; all six legs remain visible. |
| Legendary | Borrowed-Sun Solcarapace | Six monumental black-white wing-case fans, white inlay and four deep-orange convection arcs carrying pink solar phase plates; compact beetle body retained. |
| Omnic | Dawn Beyond the Broken Canopy Solcarapace | Ten huge black-white wing fans, five open orange solar arcs and dense pearlescent-pink flame channels around a vivid solar disc; central beetle and white crest readable. |

**Enemy skill direction:** Gilded Vent Strike and, at the existing second-skill
gate, Canopy Ignition. Use current Rose-format scheduled direct coefficients,
ordinary second/boss heavy replacement and existing combat RNG only.
Do not add mandatory Burn/Shield loops merely from the art.

## Social/calendar and QoL rules confirmed with this request

- Gifts are a free daily allowance, one **send per account** per reset day to
  one currently accepted friend. No sender deduction. Receiving has no daily
  cap beyond actual accepted friendships/sender limits. Claiming a gift uses
  its already-authorized attachment; event closing later does not invalidate it.
- Gift choice:1000 Prismatica,10 Null-Prismatica or10 chosen base Seed,
  including a currently active event family's Seed at authoritative send time.
  Unlaunched Moon/Sunshade Seeds are not eligible gift choices.
- Mail never expires. Persistent read/claimed state; individual and claim-all
  atomic claims, no duplicate grants. Patch notes may be informational only.
- Monthly attendance uses New York **reset-day** month, one attendance per
  reset day, next ordinal reward, manual claim. Missed days preserve monthly
  progress but break consecutive streaks; streaks carry across months.
  No paid/free catch-up invented. Reward ladder below is owner-approved.
- Bulk Masters: up to owned chests/remaining unowned choices, no duplicates,
  editable across characters, one atomic save, no automatic equipment.
- Ordinary tier material shop unlocks at any owned Element-Bearer's relevant
  current-level requirement; preserve Seed80 and existing event prices. Higher
  owner-approved higher tier prices below apply without timed stock caps.
- New-item markers cover first-ever acquired characters/captured forms/
  materials/Conduits/Masters. Seed existing legacy holdings as seen; sync
  online. Hover/focus/tap of the item clears it and parent routes; merely
  opening another page does not. Reduced motion gives a static accessible mark.
