### Values not automatically flagged as weak

- Vespera's single-target35% [Signal] and Asteriel's team30% [Moonflower]
  have different coverage; their charged Last Flares reach80% natively.
  Oryn's30% [Accord] applies to only one attack per player turn, but includes
  Normal Attacks. These are meaningful nominal bonuses, not interchangeable
  power comparisons or proven clear-rate estimates.
- Small per-stack figures must be evaluated at their **own** legal maximum.
  Only one Master is equipped: do not combine a cap-increase alternative with
  a different Master's enhanced coefficient.
- The shared4% Support role heal is existing game behavior, not a new primary
  healing ability. Leave global role perks and existing characters unchanged.
- Owner-edited Maerion reserve45%/85%, overflow Shield35%, Gauge15% and
  Caevren Load5/+10% Defense per charge and Skill2 Shield/redirection55% are
  preserved exactly. These do not certify survivability: greater redirection
  also increases Caevren's incoming load.

**Next decision:** owner selects flagged effects to revise and approves exact
replacement values. Until then, retain every listed coefficient, scope,
duration and cap. Actual pressured-encounter and paper checks remain required
before activation; this documentation review does not supply those results.

## Shared actions and role perks

- **Normal Attack:** Deal100% of Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain20 Elemental
  Gauge. Individual additions are listed under each character.
- **Defense:** Use the existing shared Defense action unchanged.
- **Skill1:**25 Gauge. **Skill2:**40 Gauge.
- **Last Flare:**100 Gauge; recover next player turn.
- Existing Skill Efficiency, Gauge Gain, Skill Power, Skill Duration,
  critical hits, action spending and recovery rules remain in force.
- **Tank perk:** +50% Defense;25% greater selection weight for randomly
  targeted enemy attacks, not25 percentage points of guaranteed targeting.
- **Attacker perk:** +100% Direct Attack and +50 percentage points Skill Power.
- **Support perk:** +50% Health and +25 percentage points Skill Power.
  Either Skill or Last Flare additionally heals every living squad member
  for4% maximum Health. The four healer identities use this existing Support
  role, rather than inventing a fourth engine role.

All allied targets are living squad members. Healing never revives. Normal
Attacks, generated equipment effects and shared role-perk healing do not
trigger character-specific skill/healing loops unless explicitly listed.
New native resources/marks are owner-specific; timers and delayed payoffs
require their source alive. No new RNG, free actions or recovery bypasses.
Each new encounter starts with zero stored resources and no pending marks,
paths or tokens. Settings preserves the current encounter's complete state;
it never resets charges or replays an expired payoff.

## Design boundaries for eventual implementation

These notes belong to design review, not repeated in every in-game tooltip.

- New native healing/Shields/buffs scale once with the caster's applicable
  Skill Power. Direct skills use the current native damage pipeline. Stored
  actual-damage amounts ([Unreturned]/[Repair]) are not multiplied by Power
  again when released. Their received-healing modifiers still apply.
- Master stat bonuses use the existing additive stat pool. Generated Master
  effects have no implicit Skill Power. Explicit changes to a named native
  ability/resource modify that native effect, which then scales normally once.
- [Signal], [Moonflower] and [Open Route] each hold one owner-specific token per
  recipient, expiring at the end of the next player turn after application.
  Only eligible native damaging Skills/Last Flare spend them; no Normal,
  generated packet or missed/invalid cast consumption. Tokens from different
  named sources are distinct and do not overwrite one another.
- [Terms]/[Accord] and [Lead] use owner-specific replacements; dead targets lose
  their designation. A [Lead] replacement caused by death grants no [Momentum].
  Replacing single-ally [Accord] also removes its associated Defense buff.
  The all-ally Last Flare application replaces, never adds to, the old buff.
- Ilyss's Skill2 permits choosing an eligible application on its selected ally;
  when none exists, healing is still usable. Her Last Flare removes [Burn]
  before [Weaken], then the oldest application of that type per ally. Removal
  is once per cast/recipient, not a full cleanse. New [Severance] from that
  Last Flare is earned after its prior stored charges are spent.
- Maerion records actual direct Health loss once per packet. Damage to multiple
  allies contributes actual losses independently; his reserve's capacity is
  enforced on each contribution. Redirected copies never contribute twice.
  His Last Flare divides the reserve before recipient healing/rounding; unused
  shares are not redistributed. Crescent Rib converts only Skill2's native
  healing overflow, not the shared role heal or another Master packet.
- Caevren routes damage after the original target's Defense/action mitigation,
  before its Shield. The routed part does not receive Defense mitigation again;
  Caevren's own applicable direct-hit reductions/Shields can absorb it.
  Redirection cannot chain or bounce, and cannot intercept AoE for free:
  each other ally's actual packet is split once. Death stops subsequent routing.
- Only the strongest non-stacking instance of the same named buff/mark applies.
  Oryn's30% offensive bonus can trigger once globally for his owner per player
  turn, not once for every [Accord] holder. [Terms] does not transfer automatically
  after a kill; the Last Flare does not invent a target if no [Terms] exists.
- Elaris's [Branch] persists until spent/replaced or its target dies. Veyr's
  [Fault] has the specified finite clock. Spending either mark does not weaken
  the spending action retroactively: capture its target/count and existing
  Defense debuff for that action, then remove it before following actions.
  Elaris's bonus applies only to the captured marked target, not unrelated AoE
  targets. Veyr's Skill2 bonus is one combined direct impact on the marked target,
  not repeated Attack Actions.
- Veyr's [Aftershock] snapshots the spent count and Direct Attack once,
  without implicit Skill Power. It is a generated Master release, not a new native cast: it creates
  no [Fault], action, role heal, Gauge-on-action or further Master chain.
- Rhaziel records the first qualifying actual absorption per ally/enemy phase,
  not a hypothetical full hit or aggregate Shield from another owner. Existing
  [Repair] adds only within its cap and pays at the next player-turn boundary,
  including Rhaziel's recovery; it is not an extra action or a fresh Skill cast.
- Aureth's Last Flare spends prior [Momentum] before moving [Lead]; a newly
  earned charge cannot increase that same Last Flare. The source/holder must
  both live for protection. No counter acquires charges from a rejected action.
- Existing Weaken safety limits, damage floors, shared extra-action limits and
  strongest-only duplicate auras remain. New mitigation combines under the
  current multiplicative non-Defense reduction and20% incoming-damage floor.
  Counts, maturity clocks, token-consumption limits, Gauge amounts and resource
  caps do not scale with Skill Power or Skill Duration. Listed ordinary timed
  skill buffs/debuffs use existing Duration; next-player-turn tokens retain
  their established bounded expiry instead of becoming permanent banked attacks.
  Defense-ignore percentages cannot exceed100%. [Fault]'s Defense reduction
  is fixed8% per stack (not increased by Skill Power), capped at40% in total.
  Repeated Normal/Skill descriptions of [Branch], [Fault] and [Reserve] describe
  the same passive receipt, not a second receipt to apply as well.
- Native and Master hooks must preserve frozen loadouts, ordered impact/reward
  events, independent origins, Settings restoration and successful-save-first
  presentation. Run level/progression, full squad snapshots, equipment legality
  and old account contents remain unchanged until an explicitly approved release.
