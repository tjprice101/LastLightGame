# Caldrex

**Infernic | Tank**

## Intrinsic Role Perk

Gain +50% Defense. Enemies are 25% more likely to select Caldrex for randomly targeted attacks.

## Passive: Banked Heat

When an enemy's direct attack damages Caldrex's HP or Shield, gain 1 [Heat], up to 3. Can occur once per enemy phase while Caldrex survives. Redirected damage and damage-over-time do not grant [Heat]. [Heat] remains until spent.

## Normal Attack

Deal 100% of Caldrex's Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain 20 Elemental Gauge.

## Skill 1: Close the Kiln

**25 Gauge**

Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 12% of their maximum HP, scaled by Caldrex's Skill Power. Taunt enemies for the next enemy phase, directing their randomly targeted single-target attacks to Caldrex.

## Skill 2: Cinder Discharge

**40 Gauge**

Spend all [Heat]. Deal 176% of Caldrex's Direct Attack to all enemies, plus 24% per [Heat] spent. Apply [Burn] that deals 20% of his Direct Attack per enemy phase, plus 10% per [Heat] spent, scaled by Skill Power and increased by 100% per 30 levels of Caldrex. [Burn] lasts 3 enemy phases; Skill Duration can extend it to 6. Reapplying his [Burn] refreshes its duration and keeps the stronger damage; it does not stack.

## Last Flare: Nothing Beyond These Gates

**100 Gauge | Recover next player turn**

Spend all [Heat]. Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 24% of their maximum HP, plus 6% per [Heat] spent, scaled by Caldrex's Skill Power. Taunt enemies for the next enemy phase. For the next 2 enemy phases, allied ability-applied [Burn] deals 25% more damage while Caldrex is alive. Does not strengthen Burn detonations or Conduit damage.

## Master: Banked-Sun Pressure Accumulator

**+60% Health | +50% Defense**

Increase maximum [Heat] to 4. Gain +10% Defense per stored [Heat], up to +40%. Spending [Heat] removes this additional Defense. The extra charge also strengthens [Cinder Discharge] and [Nothing Beyond These Gates].

## Master: Counterstroke Recoil Foundry

**+50% Health | +40% Direct Attack | +25 percentage points Skill Power**

When an enemy drawn by Caldrex's taunt damages his HP or Shield, retaliate for 80% of his Direct Attack and apply [Recoil Scorch], dealing 15% of his Direct Attack each enemy phase for 2 phases. Can occur once per enemy phase if both survive the triggering hit. Retaliation cannot critically strike; neither effect scales with Skill Power. [Recoil Scorch] ignores Defense, does not receive level or allied Burn bonuses, and ends when Caldrex dies. Reapplication refreshes it and keeps the stronger damage.

## Master: Cinderpulse Triage Converter

**+75% Health | +30 percentage points Skill Power**

When an allied ability-applied [Burn] tick damages an enemy's HP or Shield, restore 4% maximum HP to the living squad member with the lowest HP percentage. Can occur once per enemy phase while Caldrex is alive. Healing does not scale with Skill Power. Burn detonations and Conduit damage do not trigger this effect.

# Solenne

**Tranquilitic | Tank**

## Intrinsic Role Perk

Gain +50% Defense. Enemies are 25% more likely to select Solenne for randomly targeted attacks.

## Passive: Held Stillness

Store up to 4 [Stillness]. When an enemy directly attacks Solenne, consume 1 [Stillness] to reduce that hit's damage by 20%, before Shields absorb it. Damage-over-time, canceled attacks and redirected damage do not consume charges. [Stillness] remains until spent.

## Normal Attack

Deal 100% of Solenne's Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain 20 Elemental Gauge.

## Skill 1: Answer the Bell

**25 Gauge**

Deal 240% of Solenne's Direct Attack to one enemy and reduce its outgoing direct damage by 25% for 1 enemy phase. Skill Duration can extend this [Weaken] to 3 phases; only the strongest active [Weaken] applies. Taunt enemies for the next enemy phase, directing their randomly targeted single-target attacks to Solenne.

## Skill 2: Circle of Repose

**40 Gauge**

Gain 2 [Stillness], up to 4. Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 16% of their maximum HP, scaled by Solenne's Skill Power.

## Last Flare: The Last Reverberation

**100 Gauge | Recover next player turn**

Spend all [Stillness]. Deal 720% of Solenne's Direct Attack to all enemies, plus 72% per charge spent. Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 24% of their maximum HP, plus 4.5% per charge spent, scaled by Solenne's Skill Power.

## Master: Stillpoint Inertial Governor

**+50% Health | +75% Defense**

Increase [Held Stillness]'s damage reduction from 20% to 30% per charge consumed.

## Master: Quiet-Arc Shelter Projector

**+75% Health | +25 percentage points Skill Power**

After Solenne uses either Skill or her Last Flare, grant the living squad member with the lowest HP percentage an additional Shield equal to 8% of their maximum HP. This Shield does not scale with Skill Power or grant [Stillness].

## Master: Cadence Differential Drive

**+40% Health | +30 percentage points Skill Power | +15 percentage points Skill Efficiency**

When an enemy hit consumes [Stillness], restore 4% of maximum Elemental Gauge to each living squad member, once per enemy phase. When [The Last Reverberation] spends [Stillness], restore 3% of maximum Elemental Gauge per charge spent to each other living squad member, up to 12%. These grants do not scale with Elemental Gauge Gain or bypass recovery.

# Myrthen

**Botanic | Attacker**

## Intrinsic Role Perk

Gain +100% Direct Attack and +50 percentage points Skill Power.

## Passive: Turning Season

Begin each battle in [Sprout]. Using either Skill advances [Sprout] to [Bloom], then [Bloom] to [Harvest]. In [Harvest], the next Skill or Last Flare deals 35% more direct damage, then returns Myrthen to [Sprout]. Normal Attacks do not advance the cycle. A Last Flare used outside [Harvest] preserves the current season.

## Normal Attack

Deal 100% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain 20 Elemental Gauge.

## Skill 1: Reaper's Claim

**25 Gauge**

Deal 272% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to one enemy, then apply [Reaper's Claim]. The marked enemy takes 12% more direct damage from squad members' attacks and abilities for 2 enemy phases. Skill Duration can extend it to 4 phases. Only one enemy can hold her Claim; applying it elsewhere removes the previous Mark. Reapplication refreshes it without stacking. The Mark ends when Myrthen dies and does not amplify damage-over-time or Conduit damage. In [Harvest], this attack also ignores 20% of the target's Defense.

## Skill 2: Turn the Bough

**40 Gauge**

Deal 192% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to all enemies. In [Harvest], this attack also ignores 20% of each target's Defense.

## Last Flare: All That Ripens

**100 Gauge | Recover next player turn**

Deal 1,248% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to one enemy. In [Harvest], this attack also ignores 30% of the target's Defense.

## Master: Seasonwheel Harvest Transmission

**+60% Direct Attack | +35 percentage points Skill Power**

Increase [Harvest]'s bonus direct damage from 35% to 50%. After spending [Harvest], return to [Bloom] instead of [Sprout].

## Master: Seedtrace Pursuit Distributor

**+45% Direct Attack | +25 percentage points Critical Rate**

When an enemy carrying [Reaper's Claim] dies while Myrthen is alive, transfer its remaining Mark to the living enemy with the lowest HP percentage. Can occur once per round. Each newly applied Claim can transfer only once; transfer does not refresh its duration.

## Master: Reaping-Tide Recovery Dynamo

**+40% Direct Attack | +50% Health | +20 percentage points Skill Power**

When Myrthen's Normal Attack, Skill or Last Flare directly defeats an enemy, restore 6% maximum HP to each living squad member and 5% maximum Elemental Gauge to each other living squad member. Can occur once per round while combat continues and Myrthen is alive. Healing does not scale with Skill Power; Gauge does not scale with Elemental Gauge Gain. Damage-over-time and Conduit kills do not trigger this effect.
