# Caldrex

**Infernic | Tank**

## Intrinsic Role Perk

Gain +50% Defense. Enemies are 25% more likely to select Caldrex for randomly targeted attacks.

## Passive: Banked Heat

When an enemy's direct attack damages Caldrex's Health or Shield, gain 1 [Heat], up to 3. Can occur once per enemy phase. Redirected damage and damage-over-time do not grant [Heat].

## Normal Attack

Deal 100% of Caldrex's Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain 20 Elemental Gauge.

## Skill 1: Close the Kiln

**25 Gauge**

Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 12% of their maximum Health, scaled by Caldrex's Skill Power. Taunt enemies for the next enemy phase.

## Skill 2: Cinder Discharge

**40 Gauge**

Spend all [Heat]. Deal 255% of Caldrex's Direct Attack to all enemies, plus 24% per [Heat] spent. Apply [Burn] to each enemy hit.

## Last Flare: Nothing Beyond These Gates

**100 Gauge | Recover next player turn**

Spend all [Heat]. Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 65% of their maximum Health and +12% Defense per [Heat] spent for 2 turns, both scaled by Caldrex's Skill Power. Taunt enemies for the next enemy phase. For the next 2 enemy phases, allied ability-applied [Burn] deals 25% more damage while Caldrex is alive. Does not strengthen Burn detonations or Conduit damage.

## Master: Banked-Sun Pressure Accumulator

**+60% Health | +50% Defense**

Increase maximum [Heat] to 5. Gain +12% Defense per stored [Heat].

## Master: Counterstroke Recoil Foundry

**+50% Health | +40% Direct Attack | +25 percentage points Skill Power**

When an enemy drawn by Caldrex's taunt damages his Health or Shield, retaliate for 90% of his Direct Attack. Retaliation can critically strike.

## Master: Cinderpulse Triage Converter

**+75% Health | +30 percentage points Skill Power**

When an allied ability-applied [Burn] tick damages an enemy's Health or Shield, restore 8% maximum Health to all living squad members. Can occur once per enemy phase while Caldrex is alive. Burn detonations and Conduit damage do not trigger this effect.

# Solenne

**Tranquilitic | Tank**

## Intrinsic Role Perk

Gain +50% Defense. Enemies are 25% more likely to select Solenne for randomly targeted attacks.

## Passive: Held Stillness

Store up to 4 [Stillness]. When Solenne takes direct or damage-over-time damage, consume 1 [Stillness] to reduce that damage by 20%, before Shields absorb it.

## Normal Attack

Deal 100% of Solenne's Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain 20 Elemental Gauge.

## Skill 1: Answer the Bell

**25 Gauge**

Deal 240% of Solenne's Direct Attack to one enemy and apply [Weaken]. Taunt enemies for the next enemy phase.

## Skill 2: Circle of Repose

**40 Gauge**

Gain 2 [Stillness]. Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 16% of their maximum Health, scaled by Solenne's Skill Power.

## Last Flare: The Last Reverberation

**100 Gauge | Recover next player turn**

Spend all [Stillness]. Deal 720% of Solenne's Direct Attack to all enemies, plus 45% per charge spent. Grant each living squad member a Shield equal to 24% of their maximum Health, plus 4.5% per charge spent, scaled by Solenne's Skill Power.

## Master: Stillpoint Inertial Governor

**+50% Health | +75% Defense**

Increase [Held Stillness]'s damage reduction from 20% to 35% per charge consumed.

## Master: Quiet-Arc Shelter Projector

**+75% Health | +25 percentage points Skill Power**

After Solenne uses either Skill or her Last Flare, grant the living squad member with the lowest Health percentage an additional Shield equal to 20% of their maximum Health.

## Master: Cadence Differential Drive

**+40% Health | +30 percentage points Skill Power | +15 percentage points Skill Efficiency**

When an enemy hit consumes [Stillness], restore 7% of maximum Elemental Gauge to each living squad member, once per enemy phase. When [The Last Reverberation] spends [Stillness], restore 3% of maximum Elemental Gauge per charge spent to each other living squad member.

# Myrthen

**Botanic | Attacker**

## Intrinsic Role Perk

Gain +100% Direct Attack and +50 percentage points Skill Power.

## Passive: Turning Season

Begin each battle in [Sprout]. Using either Skill advances [Sprout] to [Bloom], then [Bloom] to [Harvest]. In [Harvest], the next Skill or Last Flare deals 35% more direct damage, then returns Myrthen to [Sprout]. Normal Attacks do not advance the cycle. A Last Flare used outside [Harvest] preserves the current season.

## Normal Attack

Deal 100% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to one enemy. Gain 20 Elemental Gauge.

## Skill 1: Reaper's Claim

**25 Gauge**

Deal 272% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to one enemy, then apply [Reaper's Claim] for 2 enemy phases. The marked enemy takes 12% more direct damage from squad members' attacks and abilities. Myrthen can mark only one enemy at a time. In [Harvest], this attack also ignores 20% of the target's Defense.

## Skill 2: Turn the Bough

**40 Gauge**

Deal 192% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to all enemies. In [Harvest], this attack also ignores 20% of each target's Defense.

## Last Flare: All That Ripens

**100 Gauge | Recover next player turn**

Deal 1,248% of Myrthen's Direct Attack to one enemy. In [Harvest], this attack also ignores 30% of the target's Defense.

## Master: Seasonwheel Harvest Transmission

**+60% Direct Attack | +35 percentage points Skill Power**

Increase [Harvest]'s bonus direct damage from 35% to 75%. After spending [Harvest], return to [Bloom] instead of [Sprout].

## Master: Seedtrace Pursuit Distributor

**+45% Direct Attack | +25 percentage points Critical Rate**

After using either Skill or her Last Flare, Myrthen has a 10% chance to enter [Harvest].

[All That Ripens] has a 5% chance to consume 15% of Myrthen's maximum Health instead of Elemental Gauge. Requires enough Gauge to cast normally. If the Health cost would defeat her, use the normal Gauge cost instead. Recovery still applies.

## Master: Reaping-Tide Recovery Dynamo

**+40% Direct Attack | +50% Health | +20 percentage points Skill Power**

When Myrthen's Normal Attack, Skill or Last Flare directly defeats an enemy, restore 6% maximum Health and 5% maximum Elemental Gauge to each living squad member, including Myrthen. Can occur once per round while combat continues.
