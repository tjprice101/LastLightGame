# Caldrex

Infernic Tank who draws enemy attention, protects the squad and turns incoming pressure into stronger fire effects.

- **Passive - Banked Heat:** Builds Heat when enemy attacks damage him or his Shield. Stores it for his abilities.
- **Normal Attack:** Strikes one enemy with his forge gauntlets.
- **Close the Kiln:** Shields the squad and taunts enemies.
- **Cinder Discharge:** Spends Heat to damage all enemies and apply Burn. More Heat makes both stronger.
- **Nothing Beyond These Gates:** Spends Heat for stronger squad Shields, taunts enemies and temporarily strengthens allied Burn damage.

## Master Conduits

- **Banked-Sun Pressure Accumulator:** Increases Health and Defense. Holds more Heat and gains extra Defense while storing it, allowing stronger Heat-powered abilities.
- **Counterstroke Recoil Foundry:** Increases Health, Direct Attack and Skill Power. Retaliates against an enemy drawn by his taunt and leaves a lingering scorch.
- **Cinderpulse Triage Converter:** Increases Health and Skill Power. Allied Burn damage periodically heals the squad member with the lowest Health percentage.

# Solenne

Tranquilitic Tank who prepares protective Stillness, weakens enemies and turns stored bell resonance into an offensive release.

- **Passive - Held Stillness:** Stores Stillness through Circle of Repose. Incoming enemy attacks consume charges to reduce the damage she takes.
- **Normal Attack:** Strikes one enemy with her bell.
- **Answer the Bell:** Damages and Weakens one enemy, then taunts enemies.
- **Circle of Repose:** Builds Stillness and Shields the squad.
- **The Last Reverberation:** Spends all Stillness to damage every enemy and Shield the squad. More Stillness strengthens both effects.

## Master Conduits

- **Stillpoint Inertial Governor:** Increases Health and Defense. Makes each defensive Stillness charge reduce more incoming damage.
- **Quiet-Arc Shelter Projector:** Increases Health and Skill Power. Her Skills and Last Flare add an extra Shield to the squad member with the lowest Health percentage.
- **Cadence Differential Drive:** Increases Health, Skill Power and Skill Efficiency. Spending Stillness against attacks gives the squad Gauge; spending it on Last Flare gives additional Gauge to her allies.

# Myrthen

Botanic Attacker who cycles through seasons, marks priority targets for the squad and prepares powerful Harvest attacks.

- **Passive - Turning Season:** Her Skills advance Sprout into Bloom, then Harvest. A Harvest-powered Skill or Last Flare deals extra damage, ignores some Defense and resets the cycle.
- **Normal Attack:** Strikes one enemy with her chained sickles.
- **Reaper's Claim:** Damages and marks one enemy, making it take more direct damage from the squad.
- **Turn the Bough:** Damages all enemies.
- **All That Ripens:** Delivers a powerful single-target strike. Benefits from Harvest when ready; otherwise preserves her current season.

## Master Conduits

- **Seasonwheel Harvest Transmission:** Increases Direct Attack and Skill Power. Strengthens Harvest attacks and resets her to Bloom instead of Sprout, preparing the next Harvest sooner.
- **Seedtrace Pursuit Distributor:** Increases Direct Attack and Critical Rate. When her marked target dies, its remaining Claim transfers once to another enemy.
- **Reaping-Tide Recovery Dynamo:** Increases Direct Attack, Health and Skill Power. Her direct kills heal the squad and give Gauge to her allies while combat continues.
