# Future campaign: the souls of Let and Rise (D-275)

**Spoilers / owner-approved narrative direction / NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

This records the owner's future main-story outline. It does not add dialogue,
transcription items, chapters, stages, unlocks, soul wisps, recruitable creators
or new combat elements. Current runtime behavior remains governed by
[Story and Training](story-and-training.md); the implemented founding history
remains in [world lore](world-lore.md).

## Narrative intent

The game is a search for the soul of the universe, expressed through the
competing searches for Let and Rise. Early assumptions about a good creator
and an evil rival must erode slowly. Let may not be the good figure the bearers
believe; Rise may be seeking peace rather than merely opposing him.

Reveal claims through their sources and the player's discoveries, not an
opening exposition that settles the moral question. The early papers convey
Rise's account; later discoveries complicate the founding rivalry. The final
resolution is coexistence, not replacing one absolute ruler with another.

### Chapter 1 pacing boundary

"Six stages" in the narrative outline refers to the six existing regional
sections, not replacing the current150-stage campaign with six encounters.
Rise's transcription trail spans Emberwake March, Glasswater Reach, Stormspan
Heights, Rootstone Wilds, Stillhalo Vale and Riftbound Frontier. Exact paper
text, quantity, placement, discovery timing and presentation are unauthored.

The existing final boss still does not yield a soul in runtime. The future
machine/note/wisp transition is recorded here only; do not retrofit it into
current rewards or first-clear saves without a separate implementation phase.

### Machine continuity

Current Awaken the Machines enemies remain destroyed prototypes from Let and
Rise's collaborative era. Future Let's-design wreck and Rise-infused Nyrasma
machines are specific later-story discoveries, not a global rewrite of every
machine's origin. The outline does not yet define whether the Nyrasma machines
are restored prototypes or another construction lineage.

### Creator boundaries

Rise joins the journey in Chapter4; Chapter6 uses her against Let. The owner
anticipates unique stats and elements for **both** creators, but explicitly
defers their design. Chapter7 explicitly grants Let in the future outline.
Rise's permanent recruitment timing, both kits, rarity, acquisition rules,
Infinity/Nothingness combat implementation and balance remain unspecified.
Do not auto-grant either creator or treat a soul wisp as an owned fighter.
