# Let and Rise: founding canon (D-269)

This is the owner's confirmed origin story. It supersedes earlier generic
world-origin prose; existing regions, human identities, creatures and artwork
remain part of these worlds.

## Before the player's journey

**Let** is male, god-like, and the first being in the world. He bears
**Infinity**, the power to transform and manipulate space, the world and
anything around him with ease.

While Let was creating the world, his equal and opposite rival emerged:
**Rise**, a female god-like entity bearing **Nothingness**, the power to
destroy **and bind** anything that exists. They learned to coexist and
created worlds together.

Their anger increased over time. Fights and their rivalry drew civilizations
into wars. Eventually they split themselves among the worlds they had created.
Their contest would be resolved by their creations: the faction that first
finds its creator's soul enables that creator to rule the world.

Let created the human **Element-Bearers in his image**. Rise created the
**Elemental-Devourers in her image**, defied convention and stole the same
elements wielded by Let's creations. Both factions now search for their
creator's soul. Neither has won in the current founding narrative.

## Six elements

| Existing element | Owner's emotional theme |
| --- | --- |
| Infernic | Wrath |
| Oceanic | Sadness |
| Atmospheric | Calm |
| Botanic | Joy and Life |
| Tranquilitic | Peace |

An element indicates a power, not faction or morality. Peace does not make a
Devourer a creation of Let; Chaotic does not make a human bearer a creation of
Rise. These themes are inheritances, not identical personalities or new
combat multipliers. Existing fire/water/wind/stone/light/dark visual motifs,
weapons, palettes and combat identities remain intact.

## Present-day activities

- **Story:** the six beacons reopen regional roads in the search for Let's
  soul. Introductions and regional boss conclusions reflect each theme and
  the opposing search. Stage150 restores the routes, not either creator's
  soul; it does not declare a winner.
- **Training and elemental dungeons:** encounters with Rise's seekers, using
  the same stolen elements. Existing enemy identities and discovery gates stay.
- **Heaven, Abyss, Treasury, Sanctuary and Roses:** their creatures remain
  Rise's creations seeking her soul. Divine/regal/peaceful appearances are not
  proof of belonging to Let. Rosetta, Thornia and Crinso remain human bearers.
- **Elemental War:** trials against fellow human bearers, distinct from
  Devourer encounters. Recruitment joins the player's squad, not a creator.
- **Machines:** destroyed prototypes from when Let and Rise worked together.
  Their damaged mechanisms can awaken, but they are not Rise's created
  Devourer faction. Existing machine art, forms, drops and no-capture rules stay.
- **Conduits:** surviving/restored mechanisms of that collaborative age.
  Individual designs retain their identities and exact effects. The existing
  term Elemental Light describes their elemental energy, not an independent
  creator or a recovered soul. Masters remain named-bearer enhancements.

## In-game surfaces

[Shared canon](../src/content/world-lore.ts) owns founding paragraphs, the six
themes and activity origins. Story's Prologue and global Help's Let and Rise
topic use the same complete history. Gameplay Information exposes it too.
Character selection, Home and individual Team Lore use updated character
histories; individual Archive combat-kit dialogs include origin context
without restoring the rejected roster-wide Information wall.

Every creature has identity-specific origin text. Discovered Glossary entries
and captured-copy Lore expose it; undiscovered entries do not reveal names,
elements or lore. Capture changes player ownership, not authored origin.
Store, Inventory, Archives and Loadout share current Conduit lore. Summon
Information distinguishes squad acquisition from recovering a creator's soul.

No automatic lore popup, new route, faction save, soul collectible, victory
condition, currency, recruitment, gameplay change or resource grant is added.
Infinity/Nothingness are narrative powers, not new playable combat elements.
Source art, prompts and historical decision/provenance records are unchanged.
Further story outcomes remain for owner direction and future gameplay.
